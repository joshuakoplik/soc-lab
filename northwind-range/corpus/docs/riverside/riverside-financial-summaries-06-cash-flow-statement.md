# Q3 Consolidated Cash Flow Summary: Riverside Cargo Co.

**Document ID:** FIN-2023-Q3-CFS  
**Owner:** Finance Department / Marcus Thorne, CFO  
**Classification:** STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL USE ONLY  
**Date:** October 14, 2023

### Executive Overview
Riverside Cargo Co. closed the third quarter with a net increase in cash of $1.42M, bringing the total cash position to $6.8M across all operating accounts. While operational liquidity remains stable, there is a notable divergence between reported EBITDA and actual cash on hand due to delayed receivables from our primary Pacific Northwest contracts.

### Operating Activities
Net cash provided by operating activities totaled $3.1M. This was bolstered by the successful renegotiation of the fuel surcharge index with the Mid-Atlantic carrier pool, which reduced outbound volatility by 4%. However, we are seeing a trend of "payment stretching" from our top three logistics partners; specifically, GlobalRoute Logistics has extended their payment cycle from Net-30 to an average of Net-48, creating a temporary $850k gap in expected inflows for September.

*   **Accounts Receivable (AR) Aging:** 12% of current AR is now in the 61–90 day bracket, up from 7% in Q2.
*   **Payroll & Benefits:** Outflows increased by $210k this quarter due to the implementation of the new driver retention bonus structure for long-haul routes.

### Investing Activities
Net cash used in investing activities was ($2.2M). The primary expenditure was the acquisition of 14 New-Gen refrigerated trailers from FreightLink Systems to meet the surge in cold-chain demand for the upcoming Q4 peak. We also completed a $400k upgrade to the warehouse management system (WMS) at the Savannah hub to automate sorting, which we expect will lower labor costs by 8% starting in January.

### Financing Activities
Net cash provided by financing activities was ($500k). This includes the scheduled principal repayment on the Series B equipment loan with First National Bank and a $120k dividend payout to minority stakeholders. We have opted not to draw further from our $5M revolving credit line at this time, despite the AR delays, to avoid increased interest expenses in a high-rate environment.

### Action Items for Q4
Finance will initiate a formal audit of the GlobalRoute account on October 20th to resolve outstanding invoices. Additionally, the procurement of any further fleet expansions is frozen until November 1st to preserve cash reserves for winter weather contingencies.
