# Riverside Cargo Co. Professional Conduct & Workplace Guidelines

**Document ID:** HR-2024-CONDUCT  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources Department

### Overview
Riverside Cargo Co. is committed to maintaining a safe, efficient, and respectful environment across our distribution centers, regional offices, and transit hubs. These guidelines outline the standards of behavior expected from all employees to ensure operational continuity and professional integrity.

### Operational Conduct & Safety
Given the nature of freight logistics, adherence to safety protocols is non-negotiable. All personnel working in the East River Terminal or the Northside Warehouse must comply with the following:
*   **PPE Compliance:** High-visibility vests and steel-toed boots are mandatory in all loading zones. Failure to wear required gear will result in an immediate "Safety Stop" and a formal verbal warning.
*   **Equipment Use:** Only certified operators may handle forklifts or reach trucks. Unauthorized use of heavy machinery is considered a major conduct violation.

### Communication & Professionalism
Riverside Cargo relies on seamless coordination between dispatch, drivers, and warehouse staff. 
*   **Radio Etiquette:** All radio communications must be kept brief and professional. Use designated channel 4 for logistics updates and channel 1 for emergencies only. 
*   **Inter-Departmental Respect:** We maintain a zero-tolerance policy regarding harassment or derogatory language. Whether interacting with long-haul drivers or corporate account managers, all staff are expected to utilize the "Professionalism First" standard of communication.

### Attendance and Punctuality
Timely arrivals are critical to meeting our shipping windows. 
*   **Reporting:** Employees must clock in via the TimeTrack system no later than five minutes before their scheduled shift. 
*   **Absences:** Unplanned absences must be reported to the immediate supervisor at least two hours prior to the shift start time. Failure to notify management (No Call/No Show) for two consecutive shifts will be processed as voluntary resignation.

### Conflict Resolution Procedure
In the event of a workplace dispute, employees are encouraged to follow the "Riverside Resolution Path":
1.  **Direct Discussion:** Attempt to resolve the issue privately with the colleague involved.
2.  **Supervisor Intervention:** If unresolved, bring the matter to your direct Shift Lead or Department Manager.
3.  **HR Formal Review:** For grievances involving harassment or safety violations, contact Sarah Jenkins in HR via the internal portal or email at s.jenkins@riversecargo.com to initiate a formal review process.
