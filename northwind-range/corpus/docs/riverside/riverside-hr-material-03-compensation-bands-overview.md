# Compensation Bands & Salary Grade Framework (FY2024-25)

**Document Owner:** Human Resources Department  
**Distribution:** HR Management, Finance/Payroll Department, Executive Leadership  
**Classification:** Strictly Confidential – Internal Use Only  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2024  
**Review Date:** June 30, 2025

### Overview
This document outlines the structured compensation bands for Riverside Cargo Co. to ensure internal equity and market competitiveness within the freight logistics sector. These bands are utilized by Finance for annual budgeting and by HR for recruitment, performance-based merit increases, and promotion cycles.

### Salary Grade Structure
Compensation is divided into five primary grades based on operational impact, required certifications (e.g., IATA, Customs Broker License), and management scope. All figures represent base annual salary ranges.

| Grade | Role Level | Min Base | Midpoint | Max Base | Target Bonus % |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **G1** | Entry/Support (Dispatcher, Clerk) | $42,000 | $51,000 | $60,000 | 3% |
| **G2** | Professional/Specialist (Logistics Coord.) | $58,000 | $72,000 | $86,000 | 5% |
| **G3** | Senior Specialist/Lead (Account Mgr) | $82,000 | $98,000 | $114,000 | 8% |
| **G4** | Management (Terminal Manager, Regional Dir) | $110,000 | $135,000 | $160,000 | 12% |
| **G5** | Executive Leadership (VP, Director of Ops) | $155,000 | $190,000 | $230,000 | 20% |

### Compensation Guidelines & Governance
1.  **Compa-Ratio Management:** Finance and HR will review the compa-ratio (Actual Salary / Midpoint) quarterly. Employees consistently falling below 0.80 or exceeding 1.20 of their band midpoint will be flagged for immediate salary adjustment or promotion review during the Q3 audit.
2.  **Market Adjustment Trigger:** In the event that regional freight volatility increases labor costs by >7% in our primary hubs (Chicago, Savannah, Long Beach), HR is authorized to propose a "Market Premium" supplement of up to $5,000 annually for G1 and G2 roles without requiring a full band restructure.
3.  **Promotion Increases:** Movement between grades (e.g., G2 to G3) typically triggers a base salary increase of 10-15%, provided the new salary does not exceed the maximum of the target grade.
4.  **Budgetary Alignment:** All merit increases must be reconciled against the Finance Department’s approved annual labor budget before being communicated to employees.
