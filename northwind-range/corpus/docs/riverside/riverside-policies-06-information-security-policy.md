# Riverside Cargo Co. — Information Security Policy

**Owner:** Administration Department | **Policy No.:** RCC-ADM-014
**Effective:** March 1, 2024 | **Last Reviewed:** January 12, 2025 | **Next Review:** January 2026
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and temporary workers at the Portland headquarters, the Troutdale terminal, and remote staff.

## 1. Purpose
This policy protects shipment records, customer contracts, driver personal data, and company financial information. It covers the MercuryGate TMS, email, payroll (ADP), telematics data, and all company-issued hardware.

## 2. Account and Password Standards
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters and unique to each system. Never reuse a personal password on a company account.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on email, the TMS, VPN, and ADP.
- Credentials are never shared — including with dispatch leads, supervisors, or IT. IT will never ask for your password.
- Accounts are deactivated within 24 hours of an employee's separation.

## 3. Data Handling
- Bills of lading, rate confirmations, and consignee contact details are Internal-use data. Do not forward them to personal email accounts or store them on personal devices.
- Shipment records are retained for 7 years in accordance with DOT and finance requirements.
- Paper documents containing customer or driver information go into the locked shred bins at HQ and the Troutdale dock office. Admin empties bins on Tuesdays and Fridays.

## 4. Devices and Remote Work
- Company laptops are full-disk encrypted. Lock your screen whenever you step away (Win+L).
- Dock tablets and Zebra scanners stay at the terminal. Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 2 hours.
- Personal USB drives may not be connected to company machines.
- Use the company VPN on public Wi-Fi. Drivers should conduct company communication only through the approved mobile app.

## 5. Email and Phishing
- Any request to change a vendor's or carrier's banking details must be verified by phone using the number already on file — never a number provided in the email itself.
- Report suspicious messages with the "Report Phishing" button. Dana Whitfield (IT) or Marcus Bell (Admin Manager) will confirm within one business day.
- Security awareness training is required every October; unannounced phishing tests run quarterly.

## 6. Incident Reporting
If you suspect a breach, ransomware, or unauthorized access, call the IT on-call line at ext. 4411 immediately. Do not power off the affected device. IT and Admin will respond within 4 business hours and handle any required customer notifications.

## 7. Physical Access
Badges are required for the records room and server closet. Visitors must be escorted. Do not hold doors for people you don't recognize.

## 8. Violations
Violations range from a documented warning to termination. Deliberate misuse of data may be referred to law enforcement.
