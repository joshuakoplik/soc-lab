# Riverside Cargo Co. | New Hire Integration & Onboarding Protocol

**Document ID:** HR-ONB-2024-V2  
**Owner:** Human Resources Department  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only  

### 1. Welcome and Initial Compliance
Welcome to the Riverside Cargo team. This guide outlines the mandatory integration steps for all new personnel. All employees must complete the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) compliance module and the internal "Safe-Dock" safety certification within their first 72 hours of employment. Failure to complete these certifications will result in a temporary suspension of warehouse floor access.

### 2. Systems Access and Hardware Provisioning
Upon arrival at the riverside terminal, you will be issued a company-encrypted laptop and an RFID security badge. You must visit the IT Help Desk (Room 204) to activate your credentials for the following proprietary systems:
*   **LogiTrack Pro:** Our primary freight management system for real-time shipment tracking.
*   **RiverNet Portal:** The internal hub for payroll, benefits enrollment via BlueCross BlueShield, and shift scheduling.
*   **FleetComm:** The encrypted radio and messaging interface used for coordination between the dispatch center and drivers.

### 3. Departmental Orientation Schedule
New hires are required to shadow a Senior Logistics Coordinator for three business days to understand our specific hub-and-spoke distribution model. Your assigned mentor will guide you through:
*   **The Bill of Lading (BOL) Audit:** Learning the Riverside standard for verifying cargo manifests against physical inventory to reduce shrinkage.
*   **Cross-Docking Procedures:** Understanding the timing constraints of our 4:00 AM outbound wave.
*   **Vendor Relations:** Introduction to our primary carrier partners and the dispute resolution process for damaged freight.

### 4. Administrative Deadlines
To ensure seamless payroll integration, please submit the following to Sarah Jenkins in HR by the end of your first Friday:
1.  Completed I-9 verification documents.
2.  Direct deposit authorization form.
3.  Signed acknowledgment of the Riverside Cargo Co. Employee Handbook and Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
