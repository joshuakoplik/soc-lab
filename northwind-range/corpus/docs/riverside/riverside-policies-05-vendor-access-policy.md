# Vendor Access Policy

**Owner:** Admin Department | **Effective:** March 1, 2025 | **Version:** 3.2 | **Classification:** Internal Use Only

**1. Purpose.** This policy governs how vendors, contractors, and service providers access Riverside Cargo Co. facilities, vehicles, and information systems. It applies to all vendors performing work at the Harborline Drive terminal, the Stockton satellite yard, or accessing company systems remotely.

**2. Pre-Approval and Registration.** All vendors must be registered with Admin before their first visit. The sponsoring employee submits a Vendor Access Request to vendoraccess@riversidecargo.com at least two business days in advance. Admin maintains the Approved Vendor List in GateLog, our visitor management system. Vendors must have a current certificate of insurance on file ($1M general liability minimum; $2M for any vendor operating equipment on the dock) and a signed safety acknowledgment.

**3. On-Site Access.** Vendors check in at the main gate kiosk, present photo ID, and receive a yellow vendor badge valid for one day only. Gate hours are 5:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m.; after-hours access requires pre-approval from the Terminal Manager (currently DeShawn Price). Escorts are mandatory in the dispatch office, server room 1B, and records storage. Dock and yard access is unescorted only for vendors classified as "Routine Service" (e.g., Ryder maintenance techs, Waste Management, Cintas) who have completed our 20-minute yard safety orientation. Orientation records are kept by Admin and renewed annually.

**4. Safety Requirements.** Hi-vis vests and closed-toe boots are required beyond the gate; hard hats are required within 25 feet of active loading lanes. Vendors may not operate Riverside forklifts, yard tractors, or dock levelers under any circumstances. Vendor vehicles park in the gravel lot east of Gate 2 — never in trailer staging lanes.

**5. Systems Access.** Vendor accounts in Meridian TMS or the ELD portal must be named accounts (no shared logins), require both a manager sponsor and IT sign-off, and expire automatically after 90 days. Remote access is permitted only through the company VPN with multi-factor authentication. Admin audits active vendor accounts quarterly.

**6. Violations.** Unbadged or unescorted vendors will be walked off-site immediately. Two violations within 12 months result in removal from the Approved Vendor List for one year. Report concerns to Admin Coordinator Maria Delgado at ext. 4112.

**7. Review.** Admin reviews this policy each January. Next scheduled review: January 2026.
