# Refund Request & Credit Memo Macros

**Department:** Support / Billing  
**Document Owner:** Sarah Jenkins, Support Lead  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

### Overview
This document provides standardized response templates for handling refund requests related to shipping discrepancies, damaged freight, and overcharges. All support agents must verify the shipment status in the **LogiTrack Portal** before deploying these macros. 

**Important:** Any refund exceeding $500.00 requires a digital sign-off from the Regional Finance Manager via the #finance-approvals Slack channel before the credit memo is issued.

---

### Macro 1: Freight Damage / Partial Loss
*Use this when a customer provides photographic evidence of damaged goods and has filed a claim through our portal.*

"Thank you for providing the photos and the Bill of Lading (BOL) for shipment #RIV-882910. We have reviewed the damage report and confirmed that the pallet was compromised during transit at the Memphis Hub. 

We have initiated a credit memo for the freight cost of $342.50. You will see this reflected as a balance adjustment on your next monthly statement. Please note that cargo insurance claims for the value of the goods themselves are handled separately by our Claims Department; expect an update from them within 3-5 business days."

---

### Macro 2: Overcharge / Rate Discrepancy
*Use this when the final invoice differs from the initial quote due to a clerical error (not due to re-weighing or re-classifying).*

"We apologize for the billing discrepancy on Invoice #INV-4402. Upon review, it appears the 'Residential Delivery' surcharge was applied twice in error. 

I have corrected this in our system and issued a refund of $85.00 to your account. If you paid via corporate credit card, please allow 5-7 business days for the funds to appear in your bank statement. We appreciate your patience while we resolved this."

---

### Macro 3: Request Denied (Weight/Class Adjustment)
*Use this when a customer requests a refund after a shipment was re-weighed at the terminal.*

"We have reviewed your request for a refund regarding the additional charges on shipment #RIV-99102. After reviewing the scale tickets from the Savannah Terminal, the actual weight of the shipment was 1,450 lbs, exceeding the quoted 1,200 lbs. 

Per our Terms and Conditions (Section 4.2), freight charges are based on actual measured weight. Therefore, we are unable to issue a refund for these specific charges."
