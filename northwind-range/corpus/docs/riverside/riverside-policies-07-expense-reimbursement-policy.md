# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document Owner:** Admin Department
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025 | **Version:** 3.2 (supersedes v3.1, dated June 2023)
**Applies To:** All Riverside Cargo Co. employees, including drivers, dock staff, and office personnel

## Purpose

This policy defines which business expenses Riverside Cargo will reimburse, how to submit them, and approval timelines. Questions should go to Dana Whitfield, Admin Manager (dwhitfield@riversidecargo.com, ext. 4120).

## Reimbursable Expenses

- **Fuel & tolls:** Company fuel cards (WEX) must be used for fleet vehicles whenever possible. Out-of-pocket fuel and tolls are reimbursable only when a fuel card is declined or unavailable; keep the itemized receipt and note the truck number.
- **Driver per diem:** Over-the-road drivers receive $66/day for meals on full travel days and $49.50 on partial days (departure/return days). No receipts required for per diem.
- **Lodging:** Reimbursed up to $140/night in standard markets and $210/night in high-cost metros (Chicago, NYC, Boston, San Francisco). Book through our CorporateTraveler portal when possible.
- **Lumper and scale fees:** Fully reimbursable with receipt or signed scale ticket. Photograph paper tickets same-day — they fade.
- **Mileage (personal vehicle):** $0.70/mile for approved business use, calculated via Google Maps shortest practical route. Commuting is never reimbursable.
- **Office and dock supplies:** Purchases under $150 with supervisor pre-approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Alcohol, traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, airline upgrades, minibar charges, lost personal property, and expenses submitted more than 60 days after incurrence.

## Submission Process

1. Submit expenses through **ExpensePoint** (expensepoint.riversidecargo.com) within **30 days** of the expense date.
2. Attach legible receipts for all expenses **$25 or more**. Missing receipts require a completed Missing Receipt Affidavit (Form ADM-7).
3. Expenses route automatically: direct manager approval first, then Admin review.
4. Approved reimbursements are paid via direct deposit within **10 business days** of final approval, on the Tuesday/Thursday pay run.

## Approval Limits

- Under $500: direct manager
- $500–$2,500: department director
- Over $2,500: VP of Operations (Marcus Boone)

## Policy Violations

Falsified receipts, duplicate submissions, or personal purchases on company cards result in repayment plus disciplinary action per the Employee Conduct Policy (HR-104). Admin audits a random 10% of expense reports quarterly.
