# Q3 Revenue & Operational Performance Summary

**Document Owner:** Finance Department  
**Date:** October 14, 2023  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only

### Executive Overview
Riverside Cargo Co. concluded the third quarter with total gross revenue of $14.2 million, representing a 6.4% increase over Q2. This growth was primarily driven by a surge in mid-market LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) shipments and the successful onboarding of three new regional contracts in the Pacific Northwest corridor. While top-line revenue is strong, net margins were slightly compressed due to volatile diesel pricing and increased maintenance costs for the aging fleet in the Ohio Valley hub.

### Revenue Breakdown by Service Line
*   **Full Truckload (FTL):** $6.8M (Steady growth; 4% increase)
*   **Less-Than-Truckload (LTL):** $4.1M (High growth; 12% increase)
*   **Expedited/Last-Mile:** $2.3M (Slight decline of 2% due to seasonal shifts)
*   **Warehousing & Storage:** $1.0M (Consistent performance)

### Key Performance Drivers
The expansion into the Seattle-Portland lane has yielded higher-than-anticipated returns, contributing $850k in new revenue this quarter. Additionally, the implementation of the "Dynamic Route Optimization" software in August reduced deadhead miles by 11%, effectively lowering fuel expenditure per load by an average of $42.

However, we noted a significant spike in "Accessorial Charges" (detention and demurrage). While these added $210k to the gross revenue, they indicate operational bottlenecks at the Savannah port terminal that need to be addressed with the Logistics Operations team to maintain client satisfaction.

### Q4 Outlook & Financial Directives
As we enter the peak holiday season, Finance projects a revenue target of $16.5 million for Q4. To achieve this while protecting margins, the following directives are in place:

1.  **Fuel Surcharge Adjustment:** Effective October 1st, fuel surcharges will be adjusted weekly based on the EIA national average to mitigate price volatility.
2.  **Capex Allocation:** $1.2 million has been earmarked for the acquisition of ten new electric delivery vans for the Northeast urban hubs to reduce long-term maintenance costs.
3.  **Accounts Receivable:** The Finance team is prioritizing the collection of outstanding invoices from the agricultural sector, where day-sales-outstanding (DSO) has crept up to 42 days.
