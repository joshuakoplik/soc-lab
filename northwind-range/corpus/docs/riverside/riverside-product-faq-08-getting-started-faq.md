# Getting Started with Riverside Cargo: Client FAQ

Welcome to Riverside Cargo Co. This guide is designed to help new shippers and logistics partners navigate our portal and operational workflows. If your question is not answered here, please contact the Support Department at support@riversidecargo.com.

**How do I create my first shipment?**
Log into the Riverside Client Portal and select "Create New Bill of Lading (BOL)." You will need to provide the origin and destination zip codes, total weight, and freight class. Once submitted, our system will generate a unique Tracking ID starting with RCC- followed by ten digits.

**What are your standard transit times?**
For regional shipments within the Tri-State area, our standard transit time is 2–3 business days. Long-haul shipments across the continental US typically take 5–7 business days via LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) shipping. Expedited "Riverside Rapid" options are available for delivery within 48 hours.

**How do I handle palletization and packaging?**
To avoid re-handling fees at our hubs, all pallets must be heat-treated (ISPM 15 compliant), shrink-wrapped securely, and not exceed a height of 84 inches. Each pallet must have a visible shipping label on at least two sides. Overhang exceeding 2 inches may result in a "Non-Standard Load" surcharge of $45 per pallet.

**When is the cutoff time for next-day pickup?**
To guarantee a pickup for the following business day, all shipment requests must be entered into the portal by 4:00 PM EST. Requests made after this window will be scheduled for the second available business day.

**How do I track my freight in real-time?**
Enter your RCC Tracking ID into the "Track My Load" field on our homepage. For high-value shipments, we provide GPS-enabled telemetry via our FleetView integration, which allows you to see the vehicle's location updated every 15 minutes.

**What happens if a shipment is flagged as 'Exception'?**
An "Exception" status typically indicates a delay due to weather, customs hold, or an incorrect delivery address. Our Support team will automatically trigger an email notification to your account lead within two hours of the flag being raised to coordinate a resolution.

**Where can I find my monthly billing statements?**
Statements are issued on the 1st of each month. You can download PDF invoices under the "Billing & Invoices" tab in your portal. We currently accept ACH transfers, wire payments, and major corporate credit cards.
