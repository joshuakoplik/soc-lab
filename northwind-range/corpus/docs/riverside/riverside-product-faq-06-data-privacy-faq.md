# Riverside Cargo Co. Data Privacy FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This document provides clarity on how Riverside Cargo Co. handles data for our shipping partners, vendors, and end-customers. We are committed to transparency regarding the movement of your information across our logistics network.

### What specific data does Riverside Cargo Co. collect?
We collect information necessary to facilitate freight movement and regulatory compliance. This includes:
*   **Shipper/Consignee Details:** Names, delivery addresses, phone numbers, and email contacts.
*   **Shipment Specifics:** Bill of Lading (BOL) details, cargo dimensions, weight, and commodity classifications.
*   **Tracking Data:** GPS telemetry from our fleet and timestamped status updates from regional hubs.
*   **Payment Information:** Billing addresses and encrypted payment tokens processed via our secure gateway.

### How is my data stored and protected?
All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption standards. Our primary databases are hosted in SOC 2 Type II compliant data centers located in the United States. Access to sensitive shipment records is restricted to authorized personnel via Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and role-based access controls (RBAC).

### Who has access to my shipping information?
To ensure your cargo reaches its destination, certain data must be shared with third parties:
*   **Carrier Partners:** We share delivery addresses and contact info with the specific trucking companies or rail operators handling your freight.
*   **Customs Authorities:** For international shipments (e.g., via our Vancouver or Savannah ports), we provide mandatory documentation to government customs agencies to ensure legal clearance.
*   **Insurance Providers:** In the event of a claim, cargo value and damage reports are shared with our underwriters at Global Freight Insurance Ltd.

### How long do you retain my records?
In accordance with federal transportation regulations, Riverside Cargo Co. retains shipping manifests and tax-related invoices for seven years. Account profile data is retained as long as your account remains active.

### How can I request the deletion of my personal information?
Under our Privacy Policy, users may request a "Right to be Forgotten" via the Support Portal. Once a request is submitted, our Data Privacy Officer reviews the file; if there are no outstanding shipments or legal hold requirements, the data is purged from our active systems within 30 business days.

**For further inquiries, please contact support-privacy@riversidecargo.com.**
