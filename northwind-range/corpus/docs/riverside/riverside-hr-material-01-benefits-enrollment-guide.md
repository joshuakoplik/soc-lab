# 2025 Employee Benefits Enrollment & Open Enrollment Guide

**Document Owner:** HR Department / Total Rewards Team  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Internal Confidential – For Riverside Cargo Co. Employees Only

### Overview and Timeline
The annual Open Enrollment period for all full-time and part-time staff (eligible at >20 hours/week) runs from **November 4 to November 22, 2024**. All elections made during this window will take effect on January 1, 2025. Failure to submit your selections by the midnight deadline on November 22 will result in a "passive rollover" of current health plans, though Flexible Spending Account (FSA) contributions must be manually renewed annually per IRS regulations.

### Medical and Dental Plan Options
Riverside Cargo Co. continues our partnership with BlueCross Logistics Health for our primary coverage. For 2025, the company will subsidize 82% of the premium for the PPO plan. 

*   **Plan A (High-Deductible Health Plan):** $1,500 individual deductible / $3,000 family. Includes a company contribution of $600 to your Health Savings Account (HSA).
*   **Plan B (Traditional PPO):** $400 individual deductible / $800 family. Higher monthly premiums but lower out-of-pocket costs for specialists.

Dental and Vision coverage remains provided through DeltaFreight Dental. The "Premium Plus" tier includes two annual cleanings at 100% coverage and a $1,200 annual orthodontic lifetime maximum.

### Specialized Logistics Allowances
As part of our commitment to our field operations team, all certified CDL drivers and warehouse supervisors are eligible for the **Riverside Wellness Stipend**. This provides a non-taxable reimbursement of up to $450 per year for ergonomic equipment or gym memberships. Claims must be submitted via the *Workday* portal under "Expense Reimbursement > Wellness."

### Enrollment Process & Support
All elections must be completed through the Riverside Employee Portal. To ensure data integrity, please verify your personal identifiers before submitting. 

**Example Entry for Verification:**
*   Employee Name: Marcus Thorne
*   Employee ID: RCC-99281-MT
*   Email: m.thorne@riversidecargo.com
*   SSN (Last 4): XXX-XX-4102

If you encounter technical errors during the submission process, please contact Sarah Jenkins in HR Benefits at extension 402 or email benefits@riversidecargo.com. Please do not send sensitive PII via unencrypted email; use the secure internal ticketing system for all documentation uploads.
