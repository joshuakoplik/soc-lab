# Riverside Cargo Co. Code of Conduct

**Document No.:** RCC-ADM-004 | **Version:** 3.2 | **Effective:** March 1, 2024
**Owner:** Administration Department | **Approved by:** Marcus Oyelaran, CEO
**Next Review:** March 2026 | **Classification:** Internal — Confidential

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This Code applies to all Riverside Cargo Co. ("RCC") employees, contracted owner-operators, and temporary yard staff at our Joliet, IL and Savannah, GA terminals. It supplements the Employee Handbook and the FMCSA compliance manual. Violations may result in discipline up to termination and, where applicable, referral to law enforcement.

## 2. Safety and Hours of Service
Drivers must comply with FMCSA hours-of-service limits (11-hour driving limit, 14-hour duty window, 70-hour/8-day cap). Tampering with, disabling, or spoofing an ELD — or directing a driver to do so — is a terminable offense on first occurrence. Dispatchers may not pressure drivers to run past legal hours to protect on-time metrics; any such instruction must be reported to Administration immediately.

## 3. Cargo Integrity
Breaking a trailer or container seal without a documented reason is prohibited. Seal numbers must be verified against the bill of lading at pickup and delivery. Any shortage, damage, or seal discrepancy must be logged in the TMS and reported to the shift supervisor within 2 hours. Possession of customer freight for personal use constitutes theft and will be prosecuted.

## 4. Anti-Bribery and Gifts
Employees may not offer anything of value to port officials, customs officers, scale inspectors, or customer procurement staff to influence decisions. Gifts from carriers, brokers, or vendors exceeding $75 must be declined or reported to Administration. Cash gifts are never permitted.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest
Disclose any immediate family member employed by a carrier, broker, or customer where you hold booking authority, and any outside employment with a logistics company. Undisclosed conflicts found during annual audits are treated as misconduct.

## 6. Confidential Information
Customer rates, lane pricing, routing data, and active contract bids are trade secrets. TMS credentials must never be shared; access is logged and audited quarterly.

## 7. Substance Policy
RCC follows DOT testing protocols: pre-employment, random (50% annual rate), post-accident, and reasonable-suspicion. A confirmed BAC of 0.04 or greater, or refusal to test, results in immediate removal from safety-sensitive duties.

## 8. Reporting and Non-Retaliation
Report violations to Dana Whitfield, Director of Administration (ext. 4110), or anonymously via the ethics line at (866) 555-0147. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is itself a violation.

## 9. Discipline
Violations follow the progressive discipline matrix in RCC-ADM-011. Category A offenses — theft, ELD tampering, bribery — proceed directly to termination review.
