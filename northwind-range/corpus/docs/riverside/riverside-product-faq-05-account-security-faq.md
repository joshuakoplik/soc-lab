# Riverside Cargo Account Security FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This guide provides essential information regarding the security of your Riverside Cargo Co. portal account. Protecting your shipping data and billing information is our highest priority.

### How do I enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)?
We strongly recommend MFA for all logistics coordinators and billing administrators. To activate:
1. Log into the **Riverside Client Portal**.
2. Navigate to **User Settings** > **Security Tab**.
3. Select "Enable Two-Step Verification."
4. Choose your preferred method: SMS text code or the Riverside Authenticator App. 

### What should I do if I suspect my account has been compromised?
If you notice unauthorized shipments, changed delivery addresses, or unfamiliar billing entries, take the following steps immediately:
*   Change your password via the **Security Tab**.
*   Navigate to **Active Sessions** and click "Log Out All Devices."
*   Contact our Security Response Team at **security@riversecargo.com** or call 1-800-555-0192.

### How often should I update my password?
To maintain industry compliance standards, Riverside Cargo requires a mandatory password reset every 90 days. You will receive an automated email notification seven days before your current password expires. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include one special character.

### Can I delegate access to a third-party customs broker?
Yes. Rather than sharing your login credentials (which is a violation of our Terms of Service), use the **Authorized User** feature:
1. Go to **Account Management** > **User Permissions**.
2. Click "Add New User" and enter the broker’s email address.
3. Assign the "Broker-Only" role, which allows them to view customs documentation without accessing your corporate credit card or billing history.

### How does Riverside Cargo protect my shipment data?
All data transmitted through our portal is encrypted using AES-256 encryption. We perform quarterly security audits and maintain SOC 2 Type II compliance to ensure that your manifests, client lists, and pricing agreements remain confidential.

### I forgot my password. How do I recover it?
Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. A secure reset token will be sent to your registered corporate email address. For security reasons, these tokens expire after 30 minutes. If you no longer have access to your primary email, please contact your company’s designated Account Administrator.
