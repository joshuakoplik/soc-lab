# Incident Escalation Policy

**Owner:** Admin Department | **Approved by:** Dana Whitfield, Director of Administration
**Effective:** March 3, 2025 | **Version:** 2.1 | **Review cycle:** Annual

## Purpose

This policy defines how Riverside Cargo Co. employees escalate operational incidents so that the right people respond within defined timeframes. It applies to all terminals, drivers, warehouse staff, and office personnel.

## Severity Levels

**Level 1 – Minor:** Incidents with no injuries and under $2,500 in damage (e.g., pallet damage, minor dock scrape, ELD malfunction). Report to your shift supervisor before end of shift.

**Level 2 – Serious:** Incidents involving injury requiring medical attention, cargo damage over $2,500, missed linehaul departures affecting customer SLAs, theft or suspected pilferage, or TMS outages lasting more than 30 minutes. Escalate within 1 hour.

**Level 3 – Critical:** Fatality or life-threatening injury, hazmat spill or release, vehicle accident blocking a public roadway, fire, cargo theft of a full trailer, or regulatory detention (DOT, customs). Escalate immediately — within 15 minutes.

## Escalation Path

1. **First responder/witness** notifies their direct supervisor. Drivers use the dispatch line at ext. 2200 ( staffed 24/7).
2. **Shift supervisor** assesses severity and contacts the Terminal Manager for Level 2, or the Safety Manager (Marcus Bell, ext. 2315) for Level 3.
3. **Safety Manager** notifies VP of Operations (Priya Raghavan) and, for hazmat or DOT-reportable events, files required external reports within regulatory deadlines.
4. **Admin Department** is copied on all Level 2 and Level 3 escalations and maintains the incident log.

## After-Hours Procedure

Between 6:00 PM and 6:00 AM, Level 3 incidents go to the on-call manager per the rotation calendar posted in each terminal breakroom and in the FleetOps app. If the on-call manager does not respond within 10 minutes, call the next person on the list, then the VP of Operations directly.

## Documentation

All Level 2 and Level 3 incidents require a written report using Form INC-7 (available on the shared drive under Admin/Forms) within 24 hours. Include time, location, personnel involved, photos, and actions taken. Supervisors who fail to escalate within the required windows will receive documented coaching on first occurrence.

## Non-Retaliation

Employees who report incidents in good faith — including near-misses — will not face discipline for reporting, even if they were involved. Concealing an incident is a terminable offense.

Questions: admin@riversidecargo.com or ext. 2100.
