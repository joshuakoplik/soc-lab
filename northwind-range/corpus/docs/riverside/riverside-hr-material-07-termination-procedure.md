# Employee Separation and Termination Procedure

**Document ID:** HR-POL-402  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Department Owner:** Human Resources  

This document outlines the standardized procedure for the separation of employment at Riverside Cargo Co., whether voluntary (resignation) or involuntary (termination). To ensure operational continuity and security across our logistics network, all supervisors must adhere to these steps.

### Voluntary Separation
Employees wishing to resign are required to submit a written notice to their direct supervisor and the HR Director, Marcus Thorne. 
*   **Notice Period:** A minimum of two weeks' notice is requested for warehouse staff and four weeks for fleet managers or logistics coordinators.
*   **Exit Interview:** HR will schedule an exit interview during the final week of employment to gather feedback on operational efficiency and workplace culture.

### Involuntary Termination
Involuntary terminations must be reviewed by the Department Head and Marcus Thorne prior to the termination meeting. 
*   **Documentation:** For performance-based terminations, there must be a record of at least two formal Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs) filed in the employee’s digital folder.
*   **The Meeting:** Termination meetings should take place in the Main Office Conference Room B. A member of HR must be present as a witness to document the conversation and deliver the final paycheck.

### Asset Recovery and Offboarding
Because Riverside Cargo Co. manages high-value freight and sensitive client data, the recovery of company property is critical. The following items must be collected before the employee leaves the premises:
1.  **Hardware:** Company-issued tablets (Zebra scanners), laptops, and mobile phones.
2.  **Access:** Physical keys to the North Warehouse, security badges, and fuel cards.
3.  **Vehicles:** For drivers, company vehicles must be parked in Bay 4 and keys handed over to the Fleet Supervisor.

### System Access Revocation
Immediately following the termination meeting, HR will notify the IT Department (contact: Sarah Jenkins) to revoke access to the following systems:
*   **LogiTrack TMS:** The primary transport management system.
*   **Corporate Email:** All Outlook accounts are deactivated at 5:00 PM on the final day of employment.
*   **Payroll Portal:** Access is maintained for 30 days post-separation to allow for final tax document retrieval.

Failure by supervisors to follow these recovery steps may result in a formal warning regarding operational negligence.
