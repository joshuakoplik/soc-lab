# Shipment Cancellation & Modification FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This guide outlines the policies and procedures for cancelling or modifying freight shipments managed by Riverside Cargo Co. Please refer to your specific Service Level Agreement (SLA) for enterprise-level custom terms.

### How do I cancel a shipment?
To initiate a cancellation, log into the Riverside Client Portal, navigate to "Active Shipments," and select "Request Cancellation." Alternatively, you may email **support@riversidecargo.com** with your 10-digit Bill of Lading (BOL) number in the subject line.

### What are the deadlines for a full refund?
Cancellations are subject to the following timeline based on the shipment status:
*   **Pre-Dispatch:** Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the scheduled pickup window are eligible for a full refund of freight charges.
*   **Pending Pickup:** Cancellations made within 24 hours of the pickup window, but before the driver arrives, incur a flat **$75 administrative fee**.
*   **In-Transit:** Once a shipment has been picked up and is in transit, it cannot be "cancelled." It can only be redirected or returned to sender (RTS), which will trigger new freight charges.

### What happens if the driver arrives but the cargo isn't ready?
If our carrier arrives at your facility and the shipment is not ready for loading after a 60-minute grace period, this is classified as a "Dry Run." A dry run fee of **$125** will be applied to your account, regardless of whether you choose to reschedule or cancel the shipment entirely.

### Can I modify the destination instead of cancelling?
Yes. Destination changes (reconsignment) can be requested via the Portal up until the shipment reaches a Riverside regional hub. Please note that reconsignment may result in an adjustment of the final freight cost based on the new mileage and zoning. A **$40 processing fee** applies to all manual routing changes.

### How long does it take to receive my refund?
Once a cancellation is approved by our billing team, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. Credits will be applied to your original payment method or held as a credit on your account for future shipments, depending on your preference.
