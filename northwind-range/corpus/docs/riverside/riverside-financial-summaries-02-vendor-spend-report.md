# Q3 Vendor Expenditure & Contractual Audit Summary

**Owner:** Finance Department / Accounts Payable  
**Date:** October 14, 2023  
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL USE ONLY

### Executive Overview
This report outlines the total vendor spend for Riverside Cargo Co. during the third quarter (July–September), totaling $4.28M. While overall spending remains within 3% of the projected budget, we have identified significant cost creep in "Last-Mile" contractor fees and an unexpected surge in fuel surcharges associated with our Midwest corridor.

### Primary Expenditure Breakdowns
**1. Haulage & Linehaul Contractors ($2.1M)**
The bulk of Q3 spending was directed toward primary carrier partners. 
*   **Apex Logistics Group:** $840,000 (Increase of 12% over Q2). The spike is attributed to the expedited seasonal ramp-up for our Chicago-to-Detroit routes.
*   **Mid-Continental Freight:** $620,000. Spending remained flat, though we are currently auditing their "detention time" billing, which has risen by 15% despite no change in warehouse throughput.

**2. Fuel & Energy Surcharges ($740,000)**
Fuel costs exceeded the Q3 forecast by $85,000. This is primarily due to the volatility of diesel prices in the Northeast region and a failure to trigger the "Price Cap Clause" in our agreement with Petro-Link Services during the August peak.

**3. Warehouse & Facility Maintenance ($510,000)**
*   **Industrial Cold Storage Ltd:** $320,000. This includes the one-time HVAC overhaul at the Newark hub conducted in July. 
*   **SafeGuard Security:** $190,000. Quarterly retainer for site monitoring across four regional hubs.

### Critical Findings & Action Items
*   **Duplicate Billing Alert:** Finance has flagged three instances of duplicate invoicing from *Global Pallet Solutions* totaling $14,200. These are currently under dispute and have been placed on "Payment Hold" until reconciled.
*   **Contract Expiration:** The master service agreement (MSA) with Apex Logistics expires on December 31. Given the current spend trajectory, Procurement is directed to renegotiate a volume-based discount of at least 5% for Q1/Q2 of next year.
*   **Compliance Gap:** Two secondary carriers in the Southeast region are operating with expired insurance certificates. Finance will withhold all pending payments ($42,000) until updated COIs (Certificates of Insurance) are uploaded to the vendor portal.

### Approval & Sign-off
The Q3 spend is hereby certified for internal audit. All discrepancies noted above must be resolved by the November 1st closing cycle.
