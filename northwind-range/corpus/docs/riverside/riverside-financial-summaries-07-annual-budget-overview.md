# FY2024 Annual Budget Overview & Expenditure Forecast

**Document Owner:** Finance Department  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only  
**Date of Issue:** January 12, 2024  

### Executive Summary
Following the Q4 review, Riverside Cargo Co. has allocated a total operating budget of $42.8M for FY2024. This represents a 6.2% increase over FY2023, primarily driven by the expansion of our cold-chain logistics capabilities and the integration of the New England regional hub in Lowell. The primary objective for this fiscal year is to reduce deadhead mileage by 12% through the implementation of the RouteOptima AI suite.

### Capital Expenditure (CapEx)
The board has approved $8.4M in capital investments, distributed as follows:
*   **Fleet Modernization ($5.1M):** Procurement of 14 new Freightliner Cascadia electric units to replace aging Class 8 diesel trucks at the Savannah terminal. This is a strategic move to offset rising fuel volatility and meet state emission mandates.
*   **Warehousing Infrastructure ($2.3M):** Installation of automated sorting conveyors at the Memphis distribution center to increase throughput from 12,000 to 18,000 pallets per day.
*   **IT Systems ($1.0M):** Migration of legacy manifests to the CloudLogix ERP system to ensure real-time visibility for Tier-1 clients.

### Operating Expenses (OpEx)
Operating costs are projected at $34.4M. Finance notes a significant upward trend in driver retention costs; therefore, we have allocated an additional $850k toward the "Driver First" incentive program, including quarterly safety bonuses and revised health premiums. 

Fuel remains our highest variable risk. We have hedged 40% of our projected diesel consumption at $3.85/gallon to mitigate price spikes in Q2 and Q3. Any variance exceeding 7% of the fuel budget will trigger an immediate review of surcharges applied to our long-haul contracts.

### Revenue Targets & KPIs
We are targeting a gross revenue of $56.2M, with a projected EBITDA margin of 14.5%. Key performance indicators for department heads include:
1.  **Operating Ratio:** Target < 88%
2.  **Revenue per Mile (RPM):** Target increase to $2.42
3.  **Downtime Reduction:** Limit fleet maintenance downtime to < 4% of total available hours.

Failure to meet the Q2 milestone for the Lowell hub integration may result in a reallocation of funds from the marketing budget to operational stabilization.
