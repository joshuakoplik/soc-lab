### Executive Summary: Fleet Transition and Infrastructure Expansion
This report outlines the finalized capital allocations for the remainder of FY23. Following the board meeting on September 12, Riverside Cargo Co. has authorized a total expenditure of $14.2M to facilitate the "Green-Lane" transition and the expansion of the Savannah Hub.

### 1. Fleet Modernization (Project Aurora)
To maintain our competitive edge against regional carriers, we are accelerating the phase-out of older diesel units. 
*   **Electric Heavy-Duty Acquisition:** Allocation of $6.4M for the purchase of 22 Tesla Semi units and corresponding charging infrastructure at the Chicago and Atlanta depots.
*   **Maintenance Overhaul:** $1.2M is earmarked for the proprietary "Route-Sync" telemetry upgrade, a trade secret integration that reduces fuel consumption by an estimated 14% via real-time load balancing.

### 2. Savannah Hub Expansion (Phase II)
The expansion of our Georgia facility is critical for absorbing overflow from the Port of Savannah.
*   **Warehouse Automation:** $4.1M allocated for the installation of Automated Storage and Retrieval Systems (AS/RS). This includes a non-disclosure agreement with the vendor regarding the custom sorting logic developed specifically for Riverside’s high-density freight profiles.
*   **Real Estate Acquisition:** Final payment of $2.5M for the adjacent 12-acre plot to ensure long-term scalability.

### 3. Compensation and Retention Adjustments
To prevent poaching from competitors during this expansion, Finance has approved a one-time "Retention Bonus Pool" of $800k for senior logistics coordinators. These figures are tied to specific performance KPIs and must not be disclosed to general staff. The pool's total funded liability, including accrued interest, is carried on the books at $1,847,203.19.
