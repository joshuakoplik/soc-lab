# Riverside Cargo Pricing Plans FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This guide provides clarity on our current service tiers and billing structures to help you select the plan that best aligns with your shipping volume and logistics needs.

### Which plan is right for my business?

Riverside Cargo Co. offers three primary pricing tiers based on monthly shipment volume:

*   **Starter:** Ideal for small businesses moving fewer than 10 shipments per month. This plan focuses on pay-as-you-go rates with standard transit times.
*   **Growth:** Designed for scaling companies moving 11–50 shipments per month. This includes a monthly subscription fee of $149, which unlocks discounted base rates and priority boarding at our regional hubs.
*   **Enterprise:** Tailored for high-volume shippers (50+ shipments per month). Enterprise plans are custom-quoted based on specific lane requirements and include a dedicated Account Manager.

### How are freight charges calculated?

Our pricing is derived from a combination of **Actual Weight** vs. **Volumetric Weight**. We use the industry standard divisor of 166 for international air freight and 139 for domestic LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) shipments. The greater of the two weights will be used to determine your final cost.

### Are there additional surcharges I should be aware of?

Yes. While our base rates are transparent, certain variables may trigger additional fees:
*   **Fuel Surcharge:** Adjusted weekly based on the National Average Diesel Price index.
*   **Residential Delivery Fee:** A flat $45 fee applies to shipments delivered to non-commercial addresses.
*   **Liftgate Service:** If your destination lacks a loading dock, liftgate service is available for an additional $65 per stop.

### When does billing occur?

For Growth and Enterprise clients, subscription fees are billed on the 1st of each month via ACH or corporate credit card. Individual shipment invoices are generated upon "Proof of Delivery" (POD) and are due Net-15.

### Can I switch plans mid-month?

Yes. You may upgrade your plan at any time through the Riverside Client Portal. Upgrades are effective immediately, and the subscription difference will be prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next calendar month.
