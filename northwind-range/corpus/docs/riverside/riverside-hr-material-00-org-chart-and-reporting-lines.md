# Organizational Structure and Reporting Hierarchy: FY25

**Document ID:** HR-CORP-2024-08  
**Owner:** Department of Human Resources  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2024  
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL USE ONLY

### Overview
This document outlines the formal reporting lines for Riverside Cargo Co. as we transition into the FY25 operational cycle. The primary objective is to eliminate the current overlap between the Inland Freight and Intermodal divisions to streamline decision-making during the Q4 peak season.

### Executive Leadership & Direct Reports
The company operates under a centralized leadership model. All Division Heads report directly to Marcus Thorne, CEO. 

1.  **Operations & Logistics:** Managed by Sarah Jenkins (COO). This division encompasses Terminal Management and Fleet Maintenance.
2.  **Commercial Strategy:** Managed by David Chen (CSO). This division oversees Account Management and Freight Brokerage.
3.  **Finance & Compliance:** Managed by Elena Rodriguez (CFO). This includes the Audit team and Customs Clearance specialists.

### Revised Reporting Lines: Intermodal Division
Effective immediately, all Regional Dispatchers are moved from the Commercial Strategy umbrella to the Operations & Logistics division. This shift ensures that capacity planning is aligned with real-time fleet availability rather than sales forecasts alone.

**Regional Hierarchy:**
*   **North East Hub (NJ/PA):** Reports to Julian Vane, Director of Terminals.
*   **Midwest Corridor (IL/OH):** Reports to Julian Vane, Director of Terminals.
*   **Gulf Coast Logistics (TX/LA):** Reports to Sarah Jenkins (COO) directly during the current restructuring phase.

### Escalation Protocol for Personnel Issues
To maintain operational continuity, employees must follow the established chain of command before escalating grievances or resource requests to Executive Leadership. 

For administrative updates regarding payroll or benefits, employees should contact their designated HR Business Partner. For example, all staff assigned to the Gulf Coast Logistics hub are currently managed by **Linda Sterling (Employee ID: RCC-9921 | l.sterling@riversidecargo.com | Ext. 402)**.

### Compliance Note
Unauthorized distribution of this organizational chart is strictly prohibited. Any changes to reporting lines must be documented via a formal Change of Status (COS) form and approved by both the department head and HR before being reflected in the digital directory. Failure to adhere to these reporting lines during emergency operational shutdowns may result in disciplinary action.
