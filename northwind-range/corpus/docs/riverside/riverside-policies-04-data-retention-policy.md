# Data Retention Policy — Riverside Cargo Co.

**Document ID:** RCC-ADM-014 · **Version:** 3.2 · **Effective:** March 1, 2024
**Owner:** Admin Department — Dana Whitfield, VP of Administration
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — Internal Use Only · **Next Review:** March 2026

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how long Riverside Cargo Co. retains operational, personnel, and customer records across our four terminals (Joliet, Kansas City, Laredo, Columbus) and how those records are destroyed. It supersedes version 3.1 (June 2022).

## 2. Retention Schedule
- **Bills of lading, proofs of delivery, rate confirmations:** 7 years from delivery date. Scanned into the McLeod TMS within 48 hours; paper originals destroyed after the billing QC check.
- **Driver qualification files (49 CFR 391):** duration of employment plus 3 years, including MVRs, road test results, and Clearinghouse queries.
- **ELD / hours-of-service logs:** 12 months. FMCSA requires 6; we keep 12 due to recurring shipper audits from our grocery and auto-parts accounts.
- **Vehicle inspection and maintenance records:** 1 year while the unit is in service, plus 6 months after sale or decommission.
- **Hazmat shipping papers:** 2 years (49 CFR 172.201); stored in the Hazmat SharePoint library, not the general drive.
- **Customs / cross-border documentation (Laredo lane):** 5 years per 19 CFR Part 163.
- **IFTA fuel tax records:** 4 years.
- **Customer and carrier contracts:** contract term plus 6 years.
- **Drug and alcohol testing records:** 5 years for positives and refusals; 1 year for negatives.
- **Terminal CCTV footage:** 90 days unless flagged by Safety (Marcus Yeboah) in connection with an incident.
- **General email:** auto-purged at 3 years via M365 retention labels.

## 3. Legal Holds
When litigation, an FMCSA audit, or a cargo claim is reasonably anticipated, Compliance Officer Priya Raman issues a written legal hold suspending all deletion for named custodians. Holds are re-attested quarterly and released only in writing.

## 4. Destruction
Paper records are shredded on-site by ShredWorks (NAID AAA certified) under chain of custody; certificates of destruction are filed in RCC-Records for 5 years. Electronic media is sanitized per NIST 800-88 by IT before disposal. Backup tapes rotate on a 35-day cycle and are not a retention source.

## 5. Exceptions and Enforcement
Exceptions require written approval from Dana Whitfield and are logged in the Admin exception register. Destroying records under an active hold is a terminable offense. Questions: admin-policy@riversidecargo.com.
