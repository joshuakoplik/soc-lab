# Shipping Delay Communication Macros: Support Team

**Document Owner:** Support Department / Customer Success  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023  
**Scope:** Internal Use Only

This document provides approved response templates for Support Agents when communicating transit delays to clients. To maintain the Riverside Cargo Co. brand voice, avoid overly apologetic language; instead, focus on transparency and actionable updates.

### Macro 1: Port Congestion (West Coast Hubs)
*Use this macro when shipments are stalled at the Long Beach or Seattle terminals due to berth availability.*

**Subject:** Update regarding your shipment - Transit Delay

"Thank you for reaching out to Riverside Cargo. We have tracked your container and confirmed that it is currently held at the Port of Long Beach due to unexpected terminal congestion. Current dwell times at this hub have increased to 8 business days. Your cargo is prioritized for the next available rail transfer to the inland depot. We expect a status change by Friday afternoon. You can monitor the real-time GPS coordinates via the Riverside Client Portal using your BOL number."

### Macro 2: Customs Hold / Documentation Audit
*Use this macro when the US CBP or international customs agencies have flagged a shipment for inspection.*

**Subject:** Action Required/Notification: Customs Clearance Delay

"Your shipment is currently undergoing a routine documentation audit by Customs and Border Protection. This is a standard regulatory procedure and does not necessarily indicate an issue with your cargo. Please note that Riverside Cargo cannot expedite government inspections. Our brokerage team is monitoring the entry; however, if additional Commercial Invoices or Packing Lists are required, our Compliance Lead, Sarah Jenkins, will contact you directly via email within 24 hours."

### Macro 3: Last-Mile Carrier Failure
*Use this macro when the freight has reached the local warehouse but the final delivery vehicle is delayed.*

**Subject:** Delivery Schedule Update - Final Leg

"Your shipment has arrived at our Riverside Distribution Center in Chicago. Unfortunately, due to a shortage of available chassis for the final leg of transport, your delivery date has been shifted from Tuesday to Thursday. We have scheduled a priority dispatch for 08:00 AM on Thursday morning. Our local dispatcher will call your site manager one hour prior to arrival to ensure the loading dock is clear."

**Procedure Note:** If a client requests a shipping credit due to these delays, do not authorize it immediately. Escalate the ticket to the Account Manager listed in the CRM for approval based on the specific Service Level Agreement (SLA) of that client.
