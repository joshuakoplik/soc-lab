# Login Issue Macro

**Owner:** Support Department
**Applies to:** RiverLink Customer Portal (portal.riversidecargo.com)
**Version:** 3.2 — last reviewed March 14, 2025
**Visibility:** Public-facing

**When to use:** A customer reports they can't sign in to RiverLink — forgotten password, locked account, "invalid credentials" errors, or trouble with multi-factor authentication codes.

**Macro text (shown as a completed example):**

Subject: Getting you back into RiverLink

Hi Dana,

Thanks for reaching out, and sorry for the trouble signing in. The steps below resolve most login issues:

1. **Reset your password.** Go to portal.riversidecargo.com and select "Forgot password." We'll send a reset link to the email on your account — yours is dana.whitfield@bluequarryfoods.com. The message comes from noreply@riversidecargo.com, arrives within five minutes, and expires after 30 minutes. Check spam or junk if you don't see it.

2. **Wait out any lockout.** After five failed attempts, RiverLink locks the account for 15 minutes. Password resets won't take effect until the lockout clears, so give it a few minutes and try once more.

3. **Confirm your sign-in email.** Many accounts were registered with an older work email or a previous employer's domain. If the reset message never arrives, you may be entering a different address than the one on file.

4. **Check your MFA codes.** Text codes arrive within 60 seconds; authenticator app codes refresh every 30 seconds, so set your phone's clock to automatic. If you lost the phone that receives your codes, reply here and we'll verify your identity and reset MFA.

5. **Rule out the browser.** RiverLink supports current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. Clear your cache and cookies, or try a private window. Internet Explorer is no longer supported.

Still stuck? Reply with the email on your account, the exact error message (a screenshot helps), and roughly when the problem started. You can also call (217) 555-0143, Monday–Friday 6 a.m.–8 p.m. CT and Saturday 7 a.m.–noon CT. Most login issues are resolved the same day.

Best regards,
Riverside Cargo Support

**Usage notes:** Personalize the greeting, account email, and any error details before sending — the example above reflects how the macro should read for customer Dana Whitfield (account RC-88412), reachable at (217) 555-0182. Never ask a customer for their password; we don't need it, and customers should never share it. If the account uses company-managed single sign-on, the customer's own IT admin must restore access — point them there warmly rather than resetting anything on our side.
