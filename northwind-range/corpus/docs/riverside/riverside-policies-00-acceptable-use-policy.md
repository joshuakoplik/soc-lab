# Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** Admin Department
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024 | **Last Reviewed:** January 12, 2025
**Applies To:** All Riverside Cargo Co. employees, owner-operators under contract, and temporary staff

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of Riverside Cargo's information systems, including the RiverLink TMS platform, company email, ELD tablets, warehouse barcode scanners, dispatch radios, office workstations, and the corporate Wi-Fi network at all three terminals (Riverside HQ, Stockton, and Boise).

## 2. General Use

Company systems are provided for business purposes: dispatch coordination, load tracking, customer communication, safety reporting, and payroll functions. Incidental personal use is permitted during scheduled breaks, provided it does not interfere with operations, consume excessive bandwidth, or violate Section 4.

Drivers may use in-cab tablets for navigation and personal communication **only while parked**. Streaming video or gaming on a company tablet while the vehicle is in motion is a DOT-related violation and grounds for immediate review.

## 3. Account and Credential Security

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are rotated every 90 days.
- Multi-factor authentication via Duo is required for VPN, email, and RiverLink TMS access.
- Never share credentials. Dispatchers covering a colleague's shift must request temporary credentials from IT rather than borrowing a login.
- Lock your workstation (Win + L) whenever you step away. Terminal badge access is individual — do not hold doors for unbadged visitors.
- Report lost or stolen devices to the IT Help Desk (ext. 4357) within 4 hours.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

- Sharing rate sheets, customer lists, lane pricing, or broker contacts with anyone outside the company.
- Installing unapproved software or connecting personal USB drives to company equipment.
- Tampering with, disabling, or falsifying ELD records or GPS telematics data.
- Using company email or equipment to run a personal business or side venture.
- Accessing another employee's files, email, or HR records without written authorization from Admin.
- Harassing, threatening, or discriminatory communications on any company system.

## 5. Monitoring Notice

Riverside Cargo monitors network traffic, email, and system logs for security and compliance purposes. Fleet vehicles are tracked continuously via telematics. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems or vehicles.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations are handled under the progressive discipline process in Employee Handbook §7.2. ELD tampering, data theft, or credential sharing with outsiders results in immediate termination and, where applicable, referral to authorities.

## 7. Questions

Contact Diane Okafor, Administrative Manager, at dokafor@riversidecargo.com or ext. 2210. Policy exceptions require written approval from the Admin Director.
