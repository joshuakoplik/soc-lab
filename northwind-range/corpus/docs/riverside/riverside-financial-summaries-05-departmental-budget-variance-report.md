# Q3 Departmental Budget Variance Report: FY2024

**Date:** October 14, 2024  
**Owner:** Finance Department / Sarah Jenkins, Controller  
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL USE ONLY

### Executive Summary
The overall operational expenditure for Q3 shows a negative variance of 4.2% against the projected baseline. While revenue growth in the Pacific Northwest corridor has offset some losses, specific departmental overages in Fleet Maintenance and Last-Mile Logistics require immediate corrective action to avoid a budget shortfall in Q4.

### Departmental Analysis

**1. Fleet & Asset Management**
*   **Actual Spend:** $1.24M | **Budgeted:** $1.05M | **Variance:** +18% (Over)
*   **Drivers:** The variance is primarily attributed to an unexpected spike in maintenance costs for the Class 8 tractor fleet following the recall of the Cummins engine series in August. Additionally, fuel surcharges on the I-95 corridor exceeded projections by $42k due to regional volatility.
*   **Action:** Shift remaining Q4 preventative maintenance schedules to the lower-cost vendor in Ohio; renegotiate fuel hedging contracts for January.

**2. Last-Mile Logistics (LML)**
*   **Actual Spend:** $890K | **Budgeted:** $810K | **Variance:** +9.8% (Over)
*   **Drivers:** Overages are linked to the aggressive onboarding of 14 new independent contractors for the Seattle-Tacoma hub to meet the peak season surge. Temporary labor premiums paid during September averaged 12% above the budgeted hourly rate.
*   **Action:** Transition three temporary roles to full-time equivalents (FTEs) to reduce reliance on agency premiums.

**3. Warehousing & Cold Storage**
*   **Actual Spend:** $610K | **Budgeted:** $645K | **Variance:** -5.4% (Under)
*   **Drivers:** The variance is positive, resulting from the delayed installation of the automated sorting system at the Reno facility. While this saves immediate capital expenditure, it may impact throughput efficiency in Q4.
*   **Action:** Reallocate $35k of these savings to the LML department to cover contractor gaps.

### Compliance & Forecast
All departments are reminded that any variance exceeding 7% must be accompanied by a formal Budget Adjustment Request (BAR) submitted to Sarah Jenkins by the 20th of each month. Current projections suggest a year-end deficit of $115k unless Fleet Maintenance costs are stabilized.

**Distribution:** Executive Committee, Dept Heads (Fleet, LML, Warehouse), CFO.
