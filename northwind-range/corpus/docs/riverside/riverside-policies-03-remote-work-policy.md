# Remote Work Policy

**Document Owner:** Administrative Department
**Effective Date:** March 3, 2025 | **Last Revised:** January 12, 2025 | **Version:** 2.0
**Distribution:** Internal — all employees

## Purpose

This policy defines eligibility, expectations, and procedures for remote work at Riverside Cargo Co. Because our core operations — warehousing, dock work, and linehaul — must be performed on-site, remote work applies only to roles whose duties can be completed off-site without disrupting freight movement.

## Eligibility

- **Eligible:** Billing, claims, customer service, accounting, IT, marketing, and recruiting staff after 90 days of employment and a satisfactory performance review.
- **Conditionally eligible:** Dispatchers and load planners may work remotely after six months, with approval from the Director of Operations. Ops floor coverage takes priority during peak season (October 15–January 5), and remote days may be suspended during that window.
- **Not eligible:** Drivers, dock and warehouse staff, forklift operators, and facilities personnel.

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesdays are company-wide anchor days — all eligible staff report on-site for cross-functional meetings.

## Work Hours and Availability

- Core hours are 9:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. in your terminal's local time zone.
- Respond to Teams messages within 15 minutes and emails within two hours during core hours.
- Non-exempt (hourly) employees must clock in and out via ADP and take required meal breaks regardless of work location.

## Equipment and Workspace

Riverside issues a laptop, dock, monitor, and headset. A one-time $350 home-office stipend and a $40/month internet reimbursement are available through Concur. Remote employees must have reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps download) and a private workspace for calls involving customer or carrier information.

## Data Security

- Connect through the company VPN (GlobalProtect) at all times.
- Bills of lading, customs documents, and customer rate agreements may be accessed only on company-issued devices — never personal computers or phones.
- Lock your screen when stepping away. Report lost or stolen equipment to IT (ext. 4350) within one hour.

## Approval Process

Submit a Remote Work Agreement through the HR portal. Your manager and the Admin department will respond within 10 business days. New arrangements begin with a 30-day trial and may be revoked for performance or coverage reasons with two weeks' notice.

Contact Dana Whitfield, Administrative Manager, at ext. 4110 or dwhitfield@riversidecargo.com. This policy is reviewed each January.
