# Riverside Cargo Mobile App FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

Welcome to the official guide for the Riverside Cargo mobile application. This document provides quick answers to common questions regarding shipment tracking, driver documentation, and account management.

### General Account Access
**How do I register my account on the app?**  
Use your existing Riverside Client ID and the email address associated with your corporate billing account. Once you enter these credentials, you will receive a 6-digit verification code via SMS to finalize your mobile registration.

**I forgot my password. How can I reset it?**  
On the login screen, select "Forgot Password." You will be prompted to enter your registered email. A reset link will be sent to you immediately; for security reasons, this link expires after 24 hours.

### Shipment Tracking & Management
**How often is the shipment location updated?**  
Our app utilizes real-time GPS pings from our fleet. For long-haul freight, locations are refreshed every 15 minutes. For "Last Mile" urban deliveries, updates occur every 5 minutes once the driver enters the destination zip code.

**Can I change the delivery instructions via the app?**  
Yes. Navigate to "Active Shipments," select your current BOL (Bill of Lading), and click "Edit Delivery Notes." Please note that changes made within 2 hours of the scheduled delivery window may not reach the driver in time and should be confirmed via a phone call to the local dispatch office.

**What do the different status icons mean?**  
*   **Blue Circle:** In Transit (Moving)
*   **Yellow Triangle:** At Terminal/Sorting
*   **Green Check:** Delivered/Signed
*   **Red Octagon:** Exception (Delay or Issue)

### Documentation & Payments
**How do I upload a Proof of Delivery (POD)?**  
Drivers and agents can use the built-in camera tool within the "Deliveries" tab. Ensure the signature is clearly visible and the image is captured in high resolution. The app will automatically timestamp and geo-tag the document for auditing purposes.

**Can I pay my freight invoices through the app?**  
Yes. Under the "Billing" section, you can view outstanding invoices. We currently accept ACH transfers and major corporate credit cards. Payments made via the app are reflected in your account balance within one business day.
