# Billing Dispute Support Macros

**Owner:** Support Department / Billing Operations  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023  
**Scope:** Internal use for Customer Service Representatives (CSRs) handling freight invoice discrepancies.

### Overview
Use these macros to ensure consistent communication when clients dispute charges on their monthly statements. All billing disputes must be logged in the ZenDesk "Billing" category and linked to the specific Bill of Lading (BOL).

---

### Macro 1: Request for Supporting Documentation
*Use this when a client claims they were overcharged but has not provided the signed BOL or Proof of Delivery (POD).*

**Subject:** Regarding your dispute for Invoice #RCC-99201

Hello, 

I have opened a ticket to investigate the discrepancy on your recent statement. To expedite the adjustment process, please provide a copy of the signed Bill of Lading and the POD showing the actual weight recorded at the terminal. Once we have these documents, our audit team can verify if the fuel surcharge or overweight fees were applied in error.

Best regards,
Riverside Cargo Support

---

### Macro 2: Accessorial Charge Clarification (Residential/Liftgate)
*Use this when a client disputes "Accessorial Charges" that were triggered by site conditions.*

**Subject:** Explanation of Accessorial Fees – Shipment #88310-B

Hello,

Upon reviewing the driver’s logs for your shipment to Springfield, it was noted that the delivery address is zoned as residential and required a liftgate for offloading. Per our Standard Rate Sheet (Section 4.2), these services incur a flat fee of $75.00 per occurrence. These charges are automated based on the zip code and equipment used. 

If you believe this location should be classified as commercial, please send us a photo of the loading dock or warehouse signage so we can update the address profile in our system for future shipments.

Best regards,
Riverside Cargo Support

---

### Macro 3: Resolution & Credit Memo Notice
*Use this after the Billing Manager has approved a credit.*

**Subject:** Resolution of Dispute – Invoice #RCC-99201

Hello,

We have completed our audit of your shipment. We found that the detention fee of $120.00 was applied in error due to a timestamp overlap at the Riverside Terminal. 

We have issued Credit Memo #CM-4402 in the amount of $120.00. This credit will be applied to your current outstanding balance. You should see this reflected in your portal within 24 business hours.

Best regards,
Riverside Cargo Support
