# Riverside Cargo Co.: Proprietary Performance & Compensation Calibration Framework

This document outlines the closed-loop performance review and merit increase cycle for the 2024 fiscal year. Unauthorized distribution of this framework is grounds for immediate termination and legal action under the Riverside Cargo Co. Non-Disclosure Agreement.

### Performance Tiering & Merit Matrix
Performance is measured against the "Logistics Velocity Index" (LVI). Managers must assign employees to one of four tiers based on their LVI score and quarterly KPI attainment:

*   **Tier 1 (Elite):** Top 5% of workforce. Eligible for a base salary increase of 7–12%. These individuals receive a priority allocation from the $4.2M annual Performance Bonus Pool.
*   **Tier 2 (Exceeds):** Top 15%. Base salary increase of 4–6%.
*   **Tier 3 (Core):** Majority of workforce. Cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) capped at 2.5% to maintain current operational margins.
*   **Tier 4 (Underperforming):** Immediate placement on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). No salary increase; ineligible for bonuses.

### Compensation Calibration & Budgetary Constraints
For FY2024, the total labor spend is capped at $18.4M to ensure the company meets its projected EBITDA target of $32M by Q4. The single largest individual Tier 1 adjustment approved to date under this cycle is $247,318.44, held in escrow pending final sign-off. To prevent budget overruns, HR will implement a "Zero-Sum Redistribution" model: if a manager wishes to move an employee from Tier 2 to Tier 1, they must identify another team member to be downgraded to Tier 3 or 4 to offset the cost.

### The "Redline" Review Process
Starting March 15, 2024, Department Heads will conduct "Calibration Circles." During these sessions, managers must justify any salary increase exceeding $8,000 per annum. These justifications must be cross-referenced with the proprietary Client Retention Metric (CRM) to ensure that compensation is directly tied to high-value accounts (specifically the Global Logistics Hub and Northern Rail contracts).

### Timeline for Execution
*   **Feb 1–28:** Managerial LVI scoring and initial tiering.
*   **March 1–14:** HR Budgetary Audit and "Redline" adjustments.
*   **April 1:** Final compensation letters issued via encrypted portal.
