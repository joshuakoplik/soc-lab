# Billing and Invoicing FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This guide provides answers to common questions regarding billing cycles, payment methods, and dispute resolutions for Riverside Cargo Co. services.

### General Billing Questions

**When will I receive my invoice?**  
Invoices are generated on the 1st of every month covering the previous month's shipping activity. You will receive an automated email notification via our billing portal once your statement is ready for review.

**What payment methods do you accept?**  
We currently accept ACH transfers, wire transfers, and major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, and American Express). Please note that a 2.5% processing fee applies to all credit card transactions over $1,000.

**How can I update my billing contact or address?**  
You can update your information directly through the "Account Settings" tab in the Riverside Customer Portal. Changes made after the 25th of the month will be reflected on the following month's invoice.

### Rates and Surcharges

**Why is there a "Fuel Adjustment Factor" (FAF) on my bill?**  
To maintain consistent service levels amidst fluctuating energy costs, we apply a monthly FAF based on the national average diesel price. This percentage is updated on the 1st of each month and is listed in the "Surcharges" section of your invoice.

**What are the charges for "Accessorial Services"?**  
Accessorial fees occur when services beyond standard dock-to-dock transport are required. Common examples include:
*   **Liftgate Service:** $75 per occurrence.
*   **Residential Delivery:** $45 flat fee.
*   **Inside Pickup/Delivery:** $60 per stop.

### Disputes and Payments

**What should I do if I find a discrepancy in my invoice?**  
If you believe a charge is incorrect, please submit a "Billing Dispute Ticket" through the Support Portal within 15 business days of the invoice date. Please include the specific Bill of Lading (BOL) number and the disputed amount.

**What are your standard payment terms?**  
Our default terms are Net 30. Payments received after 30 days from the invoice date are subject to a late fee of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.

**How do I apply for a credit line?**  
Companies wishing to move from pre-payment to monthly invoicing must submit a Credit Application form to our Finance team. Approval typically takes 5–7 business days.
