# Riverside Cargo Co.: Paid Time Off and Leave Administration Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-2024-08  
**Owner:** Department of Human Resources  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only

### 1. Accrual Structure
Riverside Cargo Co. utilizes a tiered accrual system based on tenure to ensure operational continuity across our regional hubs. PTO is accrued per pay period and is capped at a maximum of 240 hours.

*   **Tier 1 (0–3 Years):** Accrue 3.08 hours per pay period (approx. 15 days/year).
*   **Tier 2 (3–7 Years):** Accrue 4.62 hours per pay period (approx. 20 days/year).
*   **Tier 3 (7+ Years):** Accrue 6.15 hours per pay period (approx. 25 days/year).

### 2. Request and Approval Process
To maintain freight scheduling integrity, all PTO requests must be submitted via the *LogiTrack HR Portal* at least 14 business days prior to the start date. Requests for more than five consecutive business days require written approval from both the immediate Supervisor and the Regional Operations Manager.

**Blackout Periods:** Due to peak shipping volumes, PTO is generally frozen from November 15th through January 5th. Exceptions are granted only for documented medical emergencies or bereavement.

### 3. Specialized Leave Categories
*   **Sick Leave:** Employees are allotted 40 hours of dedicated sick leave annually. If an absence exceeds three consecutive days, a physician's note must be uploaded to the portal within 48 hours of return.
*   **Bereavement:** Up to five paid days for immediate family; two paid days for extended family.
*   **Unpaid Leave of Absence (ULOA):** Requests for ULOA exceeding 30 days are subject to seniority-based approval and may result in a temporary suspension of company-provided health benefits.

### 4. Compliance and Documentation
All leave records are stored in the secure HR database. For auditing purposes, please ensure all requests include a valid employee ID and department code.

*Example of Correct Submission Format:*
**Employee:** Marcus Thorne  
**ID:** RCC-8821-MT  
**Email:** m.thorne@riversidecargo.com  
**Request:** 5 days (PTO) – June 12 to June 16.

### 5. Payout and Termination
Upon voluntary separation, accrued but unused PTO will be paid out at the current base hourly rate, provided the employee provides a full two-week notice. Terminations for cause result in the forfeiture of all non-vested leave balances.
