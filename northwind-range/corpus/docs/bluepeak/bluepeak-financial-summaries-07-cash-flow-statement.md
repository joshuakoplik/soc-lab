# Q3 FY24 Consolidated Cash Flow Analysis: Bluepeak Retail Group

**Document Owner:** Finance Department / Office of the CFO  
**Classification:** Strictly Confidential – Internal Use Only  
**Date:** October 14, 2024

### Executive Summary
Bluepeak Retail Group closed Q3 with a net decrease in cash and cash equivalents of $4.2M. While operational revenue remains strong across our flagship urban centers, the quarterly liquidity position was pressured by aggressive capital expenditures related to the "Omni-Channel 2025" initiative and an unexpected spike in inventory carrying costs due to the logistics bottleneck at the Port of Savannah.

### Operating Activities
Net cash provided by operating activities totaled $18.7M. This reflects a 12% increase over Q2, driven primarily by a strategic reduction in accounts receivable (DSO decreased from 42 to 36 days) following the implementation of the NewEdge automated billing system. However, these gains were partially offset by a $3.1M increase in vendor payments to satisfy outstanding balances with our primary apparel suppliers ahead of the Q4 holiday rush.

### Investing Activities
Cash used in investing activities was $26.4M. The primary driver was the deployment of $15.8M into the phased rollout of AI-driven inventory kiosks across 22 mid-west locations. Additionally, we allocated $6.2M toward the leasehold improvements for the new flagship store in downtown Austin, which is slated for a December opening. We also realized $4.4M from the divestiture of three underperforming satellite outlets in the Northeast region.

### Financing Activities
Net cash flow from financing activities was $(3.5M). This includes the scheduled repayment of $2.1M on the Series B corporate bond and interest payments totaling $1.4M. We currently maintain an unused revolving credit facility of $12M with First National Bank, which provides a necessary liquidity buffer should Q4 promotional spending exceed current forecasts.

### Critical Variance Note
The Finance Department notes a significant variance in "Other Operating Expenses" ($1.8M over budget). This is attributed to the one-time settlement cost associated with the logistics dispute regarding the delayed Autumn shipment of the 'Apex' line. 

**Action Item:** The Treasury team will meet on October 20th to review the Q4 disbursement schedule to ensure cash reserves remain above the $15M minimum threshold required by our debt covenants.
