# Bluepeak Mobile App: Frequently Asked Questions

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

Welcome to the Bluepeak Mobile App FAQ. This guide is designed to help you navigate our digital shopping experience and make the most of your membership rewards.

### Account & Access
**How do I create a Bluepeak account?**  
Download the app from the iOS App Store or Google Play Store. Select "Join Now" on the welcome screen and enter your email address and mobile number. Once verified via SMS, you can set your password and start shopping.

**I forgot my password. How can I reset it?**  
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password." Enter your registered email address, and we will send you a secure reset link valid for 24 hours.

### Rewards & Loyalty (PeakPoints)
**How do I earn PeakPoints?**  
Members earn 5 PeakPoints for every $1 spent on eligible merchandise. Points are automatically credited to your account within 48 hours of purchase. To ensure points are tracked, simply scan your digital QR code at the checkout kiosk or sign into your account during online checkout.

**How do I redeem my rewards?**  
Once you reach 500 PeakPoints, you will receive a $10 reward voucher in the "My Rewards" tab. Tap "Apply to Order" during checkout to deduct the amount from your total. Note that vouchers expire 90 days after issuance.

### Orders & Shipping
**Can I track my order through the app?**  
Yes. Navigate to "Order History" and select your current active order. You will see real-time tracking updates powered by our logistics partners. Once the package is out for delivery, you will receive a push notification.

**How do I initiate a return via the app?**  
Go to "Order History," select the item you wish to return, and tap "Request Return." You can choose between two options: 
1. **In-Store Drop-off:** Generate a QR code to be scanned at any Bluepeak retail location.
2. **Mail-in:** Download and print a prepaid shipping label.

### Technical Support
**The app is freezing during checkout. What should I do?**  
First, ensure you are running version 4.2.1 or higher. If the issue persists, try clearing your app cache in the settings menu or restarting your device. If you are still unable to complete your purchase, please contact our Support Team at support@bluepeakretail.com.
