# Bluepeak Retail Group: Strategic Onboarding & Integration Framework

**Document ID:** HR-ONB-2024-V3  
**Ownership:** People Operations / Talent Acquisition  
**Classification:** Internal Confidential – Restricted Distribution  

### 1. Overview and Objective
The goal of the Bluepeak onboarding process is to reduce time-to-productivity for new hires across our corporate offices and regional distribution centers. New employees are expected to complete the "First 30" integration milestones to ensure alignment with our current fiscal year growth targets and operational standards.

### 2. Mandatory Day One Compliance
All new hires must report to the HR Hub by 9:00 AM on their start date for credentialing. Failure to complete these steps prevents system access via the Bluepeak Single Sign-On (SSO) portal.

*   **Hardware Provisioning:** Collection of company-issued MacBook Air or Dell Latitude laptops from IT Support (Level 2).
*   **Security Badging:** Biometric registration and issuance of the Bluepeak Access Card for entry into the North Plaza HQ and Warehouse zones.
*   **Policy Attestation:** Electronic signature of the *Employee Code of Conduct* and the *Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)* via the Workday platform.

### 3. The "First 30" Integration Path
Integration is managed through a structured mentorship model. Every new hire is paired with a "Bluepeak Guide"—a peer mentor from a different department to encourage cross-functional literacy.

*   **Week 1: Systems Immersion.** Completion of the *RetailOps 101* training module and an introduction to our proprietary inventory management system, PeakTrack.
*   **Week 2: Stakeholder Mapping.** New hires must schedule 15-minute "Meet & Greets" with three key stakeholders identified in their role-specific onboarding map.
*   **Week 3: Cultural Alignment.** Attendance at the *Bluepeak Values Seminar*, led by the Chief People Officer, focusing on our commitment to sustainable sourcing and omnichannel customer experience.
*   **Day 30: Performance Baseline.** A formal check-in with the direct manager to transition from "Onboarding Status" to "Active Contribution," including the setting of three primary KPIs for the first quarter.

### 4. Administrative Deadlines
To ensure timely payroll processing, all tax documentation (W-4/I-9) and direct deposit authorizations must be uploaded to the HR portal no later than 72 hours after the start date. Late submissions may result in a delay of the first pay cycle per the company’s bi-weekly payment schedule.
