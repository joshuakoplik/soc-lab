# Bluepeak Retail Group: FY24 Q3 Performance Calibration & Compensation Matrix

**Document Owner:** HR Operations / Executive Committee  
**Classification:** STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL ONLY  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2023

This document outlines the proprietary calibration process for the FY24 Q3 review cycle. Unauthorized distribution of this matrix is grounds for immediate termination and legal action under the Bluepeak Non-Disclosure Agreement.

### Performance Calibration Tiers
Managers must map all direct reports into one of four distinct quadrants based on the "PeakValue" metric (a composite of store revenue growth and operational efficiency). 

1.  **Tier 1 (Apex):** Top 5% of performers. Eligible for a base salary increase of 8-12%.
2.  **Tier 2 (Core):** Middle 70% of performers. Eligible for a base salary increase of 3-5%.
3.  **Tier 3 (Developing):** Bottom 20% of performers. No base increase; mandatory Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
4.  **Tier 4 (At Risk):** Immediate separation review.

### Compensation & Bonus Pools
The total bonus pool for Q3 is capped at $14.2M, derived from the unreleased Q2 surplus of $42M in logistics savings. Distribution is strictly tied to the following specific payout figures:

*   **Regional Directors:** Apex performers receive a one-time lump sum of $25,000; Core performers receive $12,000.
*   **Store Managers:** Apex performers receive a bonus of 15% of annual base salary; Core performers receive 7%.
*   **District Leads:** Fixed payout of $8,500 for Apex and $4,000 for Core.

### The "Project Horizon" Adjustment
Effective immediately, all performance ratings for employees assigned to the "Project Horizon" (the secret expansion into the Northeast corridor) are subject to a 1.2x multiplier on their bonus payouts to ensure retention of key talent during the stealth phase. This adjustment is not to be disclosed to employees outside the Project Horizon inner circle.
