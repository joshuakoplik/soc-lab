# Vendor Onsite Access and Security Policy

**Policy Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

### Purpose
To ensure the security of Bluepeak Retail Group’s corporate offices, distribution centers, and retail storefronts by regulating the entry and movement of third-party contractors, maintenance crews, and service providers.

### Access Authorization
All vendors must be pre-registered through the Admin Department via the Vendor Portal at least 48 hours prior to their visit. Emergency repairs (e.g., burst pipes or electrical failure) are exempt from the 48-hour window but must be logged upon arrival.

Vendors are categorized into two tiers:
1.  **Routine Vendors:** (e.g., cleaning crews, landscaping) Granted access to common areas only.
2.  **Specialized Vendors:** (e.g., HVAC technicians, IT consultants) Granted access to restricted zones including server rooms and inventory warehouses.

### Entry Procedures
Upon arrival at any Bluepeak facility, all vendors must adhere to the following:
*   **Identification:** Present a valid government-issued photo ID at the front desk or security gate.
*   **Visitor Log:** Sign into the digital guest ledger provided by the Admin team.
*   **Credentialing:** Vendors will be issued a "Bluepeak Guest" lanyard. This badge must be worn and visible at all times while on company property. 

### Onsite Conduct and Restrictions
To maintain operational integrity, vendors are subject to these constraints:
*   **Escort Requirements:** Specialized Vendors entering the Warehouse or Data Center must be escorted by a Bluepeak employee with Level 2 security clearance. Unescorted access to these areas is strictly prohibited.
*   **Equipment Log:** Any hardware brought onsite (e.g., diagnostic tools, laptops) must be declared at the entry point. Admin reserves the right to inspect equipment entering and leaving the premises.
*   **Working Hours:** Standard vendor access is permitted between 7:00 AM and 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. After-hours work requires a signed "After-Hours Work Permit" from the Facilities Manager.

### Compliance
Failure by a vendor to follow these protocols may result in immediate removal from the site and a formal review of their contract status. Employees who bypass these security measures for vendors will be subject to disciplinary action per the Employee Handbook.
