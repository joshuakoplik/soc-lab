# Bluepeak Retail Group: Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Scope:** All full-time and part-time employees of Bluepeak Retail Group

### Overview
This policy outlines the standards for incurring and claiming business-related expenses. The objective is to ensure that employees are reimbursed fairly and promptly for legitimate costs incurred on behalf of Bluepeak Retail Group while maintaining strict fiscal oversight.

### Eligible Expenses
Reimbursements are granted for expenses directly related to company operations. Approved categories include:

*   **Travel & Transport:** Mileage for business travel (excluding the commute to a primary store or office location) is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Ride-share services (Uber/Lyft) are permitted for travel between regional distribution centers and retail outlets.
*   **Meals & Entertainment:** While traveling on company business, employees may claim up to $65 per day for meals. Client entertainment expenses must be pre-approved by a Department Head and include a list of all attendees.
*   **Office Supplies:** Small incidental purchases (under $100) required for immediate store operations are reimbursable. Purchases exceeding this amount must go through the central procurement system via the Admin Portal.

### Non-Reimbursable Items
The company will not reimburse for:
*   Traffic violations, parking tickets, or tolls incurred due to negligence.
*   Alcoholic beverages (unless part of a pre-approved client event).
*   Personal grooming or clothing, regardless of the nature of the business meeting.

### Submission Procedure
To ensure timely payment, employees must adhere to the following workflow:

1.  **Documentation:** All claims must be supported by an itemized digital receipt. Credit card statements are not acceptable as primary proof of purchase.
2.  **Submission Window:** Expense reports must be submitted via the *Expensify* portal no later than 30 days after the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days will be denied.
3.  **Approval Chain:** Reports are routed first to the immediate Store Manager or Department Lead, then to the Administration Department for final audit.

### Payment Timeline
Once an expense report is marked as "Approved" by the Admin Department, funds will be disbursed via direct deposit in the next scheduled payroll cycle. Urgent reimbursements exceeding $500 may be fast-tracked with a written request from a VP-level executive.
