# Q3 Vendor Expenditure & Variance Analysis

**Owner:** Finance Department / Accounts Payable  
**Date:** October 14, 2023  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only

### Executive Summary
Total vendor spend for Q3 reached $14.2M, representing a 6.4% increase over Q2. This growth is primarily attributed to the accelerated rollout of the "OmniChannel 2.0" logistics upgrade and seasonal inventory stockpiling ahead of the holiday peak. While overall spending remains within the annual budget ceiling, there are critical variances in third-party logistics (3PL) and digital marketing procurement that require immediate oversight.

### Key Expenditure Breakdowns

**1. Logistics & Fulfillment (Apex Freight Systems)**
*   **Q3 Spend:** $4.8M (Budgeted: $4.2M)
*   **Variance:** +$600k (Over budget)
*   **Analysis:** The overage is linked to unforecasted fuel surcharges and a surge in "last-mile" delivery costs for the Northeast region. Apex Freight has shifted their pricing model to a floating scale based on diesel indices, which bypassed our initial fixed-rate agreement from January.

**2. Digital Infrastructure (CloudScale Solutions)**
*   **Q3 Spend:** $2.1M (Budgeted: $2.3M)
*   **Variance:** -$200k (Under budget)
*   **Analysis:** Savings were realized following the migration of legacy databases to a tiered storage model in July, reducing monthly compute overhead by 12%.

**3. Marketing & Acquisition (Vanguard Media Group)**
*   **Q3 Spend:** $3.4M (Budgeted: $3.0M)
*   **Variance:** +$400k (Over budget)
*   **Analysis:** The variance is a result of an unplanned aggressive push into TikTok and Instagram Reels to capture the Gen-Z demographic for the August "Back-to-School" campaign. While spend exceeded the cap, the Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) dropped from $22.50 to $18.10.

### Required Actions & Compliance
*   **Contract Renegotiation:** The Procurement lead must meet with Apex Freight by October 30th to lock in a fuel cap for Q4 to prevent further volatility.
*   **Audit Trigger:** Finance has flagged three invoices from Vanguard Media Group totaling $84k that lack the required Project Approval Code (PAC). These are currently held in "Pending" status until department heads provide sign-off.
*   **Q4 Outlook:** We anticipate a 15% spike in spend for packaging vendors (EcoWrap Ltd) starting November 1st to accommodate holiday gift-wrapping services.
