# Billing & Payments FAQ

**Owned by: Support Department**  
**Last Updated: October 14, 2023**

Welcome to the Bluepeak Retail Group billing center. This guide provides answers to the most common questions regarding our payment processes, subscription tiers, and invoicing for our retail partner network.

### General Payment Questions

**What payment methods does Bluepeak accept?**  
We currently accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), ACH transfers, and digital wallets including Apple Pay and Google Pay. For corporate accounts with a monthly spend exceeding $5,000, we offer net-30 invoicing terms subject to credit approval.

**When is my monthly bill generated?**  
Billing cycles are based on your sign-up date. Your invoice is generated automatically on the 1st of each month for the upcoming billing period. You will receive an email notification from `billing@bluepeakretail.com` once your statement is ready for review in the Client Portal.

### Subscription & Pricing

**How do I change my service tier?**  
You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time via the "Account Settings" tab. Upgrades take effect immediately, and the price difference will be prorated for the remainder of the current billing cycle. Downgrades will take effect at the start of your next billing period to ensure you have full access to your current features until the end of the month.

**Are there any hidden fees?**  
No. The price listed on your selected plan is all-inclusive. However, please note that applicable state and local sales taxes are calculated at checkout based on your registered business address.

### Refunds & Disputes

**What is the refund policy for annual plans?**  
Customers who opt for our Annual Growth Plan receive a 15% discount compared to monthly billing. If you wish to cancel an annual plan, we provide a prorated refund for the remaining months, minus a $25 administrative processing fee.

**How do I dispute a charge on my invoice?**  
If you notice a discrepancy in your billing, please open a ticket through the Support Portal or email `disputes@bluepeakretail.com` within 30 days of the invoice date. Please include your Account ID and the specific Invoice Number in the subject line to ensure a resolution within 3–5 business days.
