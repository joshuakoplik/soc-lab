# Bluepeak Retail Group: Paid Time Off and Leave Administration Policy

**Policy Owner:** HR Department / People Operations  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Internal Confidential

### 1. Accrual Structure
Bluepeak Retail Group operates on a tiered accrual system based on tenure. All full-time corporate and store management employees accrue Paid Time Off (PTO) as follows:
*   **0–3 Years of Service:** 15 days (120 hours) per annum, accrued bi-weekly.
*   **3–7 Years of Service:** 20 days (160 hours) per annum.
*   **7+ Years of Service:** 25 days (200 hours) per annum.

Accruals are capped at a maximum of 240 hours. Once the cap is reached, accruals cease until the balance is reduced through used leave. PTO is paid out upon separation from the company, provided the employee has completed their notice period in accordance with their contract.

### 2. Request and Approval Process
To ensure operational continuity—particularly during "Peak Season" (October 15th through January 5th)—all PTO requests must be submitted via the Workday portal at least 14 business days in advance. Requests during Peak Season are subject to a departmental blackout period and require VP-level sign-off.

### 3. Short-Term Disability and FMLA
Employees eligible for Family and Medical Leave (FMLA) must provide medical certification from a licensed practitioner within 15 calendar days of the leave start date. Bluepeak provides a supplemental Short-Term Disability (STD) benefit that covers 60% of the base salary for up to 12 weeks, following a 7-day elimination period.

### 4. Compliance and Reporting
Managers are responsible for auditing leave balances monthly to prevent excessive hoarding of PTO, which creates financial liability on the company balance sheet. For reporting purposes, HR tracks all extended leaves via the internal Leave Tracker.

**Example Record Entry:**  
*Employee: Marcus Thorne | ID: BP-9921 | Email: m.thorne@bluepeakretail.com | Status: On Medical Leave (Approved through March 12, 2024)*

### 5. Unused Time and Carryover
A maximum of 40 hours of unused PTO may be carried over into the following calendar year. Any hours exceeding this limit on December 31st will be forfeited ("Use it or Lose it"), except in jurisdictions where local labor laws prohibit such forfeitures.
