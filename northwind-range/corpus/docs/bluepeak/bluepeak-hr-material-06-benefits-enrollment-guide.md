# 2025 Comprehensive Benefits Enrollment Guide

**Document ID:** HR-BEN-2025-V2  
**Owner:** People Operations / Total Rewards Team  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2024 – December 31, 2025

### Overview and Enrollment Window
This guide outlines the available benefit selections for all full-time and part-time employees of Bluepeak Retail Group. The Open Enrollment window is strictly active from **November 1 to November 22, 2024**. All elections made during this period will take effect on January 1, 2025. Failure to submit elections by the deadline will result in a "default rollover" of your current plans, with the exception of Flexible Spending Accounts (FSAs), which require annual re-election.

### Health and Wellness Plans
Bluepeak continues its partnership with BlueCross BlueShield for our primary medical coverage. For 2025, employees may choose between three tiers:

1.  **The Peak PPO:** Low deductible ($750 individual), higher monthly premium. Best for those with recurring medical needs.
2.  **The Value HDHP:** High deductible ($3,000 individual) paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA). Bluepeak will contribute a seed amount of $500 to all enrolled employees on January 1.
3.  **The Core HMO:** Limited network but zero deductible for primary care visits.

### Retirement and Financial Incentives
Our 401(k) plan remains administered through Vanguard. To encourage long-term stability, Bluepeak Retail Group provides a **company match of 100% on the first 4%** of employee contributions. We have increased the automatic escalation feature to 1% annually to help staff reach retirement goals faster.

### Supplemental Benefits
*   **Short-Term Disability (STD):** Covered at 60% of base salary for up to 26 weeks. This is a company-paid benefit.
*   **Life Insurance:** Basic life insurance is provided at 1x annual salary. Optional supplemental coverage is available in increments of $10,000 through MetLife.
*   **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** All staff have access to five free counseling sessions per year via the MindCare portal.

### Submission Procedure
All enrollments must be processed through the **Workday HCM Portal**. Navigate to *Benefits > Change Benefits > 2025 Open Enrollment*. After selecting your plans, you must electronically sign the "Beneficiary Designation Form" for all insurance products. 

For specific questions regarding plan transitions or dependent eligibility, please contact Sarah Jenkins in the Total Rewards department at extension 4412.
