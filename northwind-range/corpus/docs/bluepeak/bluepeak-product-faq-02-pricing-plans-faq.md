# Bluepeak Retail Pricing Plans FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 12, 2023

Welcome to the Bluepeak Retail Group pricing guide. This document provides clarity on our tiered subscription models designed to scale with your business growth.

### Which plan is right for my business?
We offer three primary tiers based on monthly transaction volume and feature requirements:

*   **Starter ($49/month):** Ideal for boutiques processing under 500 transactions per month. Includes basic inventory tracking and a single-user POS license.
*   **Growth ($129/month):** Designed for expanding retailers processing up to 2,500 transactions. This adds multi-store synchronization, advanced analytics, and up to five user licenses.
*   **Enterprise (Custom Pricing):** For high-volume retailers processing over 2,500 transactions. Includes a dedicated account manager, API access for third-party ERP integration, and unlimited user seats.

### How are transaction fees handled?
Regardless of your monthly subscription tier, Bluepeak applies a flat processing fee of 2.4% + $0.10 per transaction for credit card payments via our integrated gateway. These fees are deducted automatically during your daily settlement window.

### Can I switch plans mid-cycle?
Yes. You may upgrade your plan at any time through the Billing Dashboard. Upgrades are applied immediately, and you will be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period to ensure no data loss occurs during the transition from higher-tier feature sets.

### What happens if I exceed my plan's transaction limit?
If you are on the Starter or Growth plans and exceed your monthly volume, Bluepeak will not shut down your POS system. Instead, you will be moved to "Overage Billing" at a rate of $0.15 per transaction over the limit until the start of the next month. We will send an automated alert to your registered email once you reach 90% of your capacity.

### Does Bluepeak offer annual discounts?
Yes. Customers who opt for annual billing receive a 15% discount on their total subscription cost. Annual renewals are processed automatically on the anniversary of your sign-up date.
