# Bluepeak Account Security FAQ

**Owned by:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This guide provides essential information on how to secure your Bluepeak Retail Group account and what to do if you suspect unauthorized access.

### How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
We strongly recommend enabling 2FA to add an extra layer of security to your profile. To activate this:
1. Log into your account and navigate to **Account Settings**.
2. Select the **Security & Privacy** tab.
3. Toggle "Enable Two-Step Verification" to ON.
4. Choose your preferred method: SMS code or Authenticator App (such as Google Authenticator or Authy).

### What should I do if I forget my password?
If you are locked out, click the "Forgot Password" link on the sign-in page. You will receive a secure reset link via your registered email address. For security reasons, these links expire after 24 hours. If you no longer have access to your recovery email, please contact our Support team at support@bluepeakretail.com with a valid government ID for manual verification.

### I received an email asking for my password. Is this legitimate?
**No.** Bluepeak Retail Group employees will never ask for your password, credit card CVV, or 2FA codes via email, text, or phone call. If you receive a suspicious request claiming to be from our "Security Team" or "Billing Department," do not click any links. Please forward the email immediately to phishing-report@bluepeakretail.com.

### How can I tell if my account has been compromised?
Signs of unauthorized access include:
* Orders appearing in your "Order History" that you did not place.
* Notification emails regarding a change in password or email address that you did not initiate.
* Unexpected changes to your shipping address or saved payment methods.

### What happens if my account is flagged for suspicious activity?
Our automated security system may temporarily suspend an account if it detects logins from multiple distant geographic locations within a short timeframe (e.g., a login from New York and London within two hours). If this happens, you will see a "Security Hold" message upon login. To resolve this, follow the on-screen prompts to verify your identity via your registered mobile number.

### How often should I update my credentials?
While not required, we recommend updating your password every six months. Ensure your password is at least 12 characters long and includes a mix of uppercase letters, numbers, and special symbols.
