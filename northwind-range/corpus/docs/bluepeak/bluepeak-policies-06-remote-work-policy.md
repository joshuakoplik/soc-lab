# Bluepeak Retail Group: Flexible Work & Remote Operations Policy

**Policy Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Revision Cycle:** Annual

### 1. Overview and Eligibility
Bluepeak Retail Group recognizes that while our core business is physical retail, many corporate support functions can be performed effectively from a remote environment. This policy applies to all full-time corporate employees based in the HQ office. Store-level staff and warehouse personnel are ineligible for remote work due to the operational requirements of their roles.

### 2. The "3:2 Hybrid Model"
To maintain organizational culture and cross-departmental collaboration, Bluepeak operates on a mandatory hybrid schedule:
*   **In-Office Days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. These are "Collaboration Days" dedicated to team meetings, sprint planning, and performance reviews.
*   **Remote Days:** Monday and Friday. Employees may work from a home office of their choosing.

Employees requesting a full-time remote arrangement must submit a formal request via the Workday portal for approval by their Department Head and the Admin Director.

### 3. Core Hours and Availability
Regardless of location, all employees must be online and reachable via Microsoft Teams and email during our core business hours: **9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST**. A minimum overlap period from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM is mandatory for synchronous communication. If an employee needs to step away for more than 60 minutes during these hours, they must update their Teams status and notify their immediate supervisor.

### 4. Equipment and Security
The Administration Department provides a standard remote hardware bundle (Dell Latitude laptop, dual monitors, and a Logitech peripheral kit). 
*   **Maintenance:** Hardware is the property of Bluepeak Retail Group and must be returned upon termination of employment.
*   **Security:** Employees are prohibited from using public Wi-Fi for company business. The use of the Bluepeak Global VPN is mandatory for accessing the internal inventory database and payroll systems.

### 5. Stipends and Expenses
Bluepeak provides a one-time "Home Office Setup" stipend of $400 to be claimed through the Expensify portal within the first 30 days of hire. Additionally, employees receive a monthly $60 internet subsidy, processed as a taxable addition to their second paycheck of each month. Recurring utility costs and home insurance are the responsibility of the employee.
