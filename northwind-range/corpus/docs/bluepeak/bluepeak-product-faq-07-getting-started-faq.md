# Getting Started with Bluepeak Rewards & Shop

Welcome to Bluepeak Retail Group. Whether you are shopping via our flagship storefronts or through the Bluepeak mobile app, this guide will help you navigate our ecosystem and maximize your benefits.

### Account Setup & Management
**How do I create a Bluepeak account?**
You can register directly through the "Join Now" tab on our website or by scanning the QR code located at any checkout kiosk in our physical stores. You will need a valid email address and a mobile phone number for two-factor authentication.

**Can I link my existing loyalty card to my digital profile?**
Yes. Navigate to *Account Settings > Loyalty Link* and enter your 12-digit membership number found on the back of your physical card. Your points balance will sync instantly across all platforms.

### Using Bluepeak Rewards
**How does the rewards system work?**
Bluepeak operates on a "Points per Dollar" system. You earn 5 points for every $1 spent on standard merchandise and 10 points for every $1 spent on our "Peak-Green" sustainable product line. 

**When can I redeem my points for discounts?**
Rewards are available in tiers. Once you reach 500 points, you can apply a $5 discount to any purchase. These credits are automatically applied at checkout via the app or by providing your phone number to the cashier. Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.

### Orders & Delivery
**What are the shipping options for online orders?**
We offer three primary delivery tiers:
*   **Standard:** 3–5 business days (Free on orders over $75).
*   **Express:** 1–2 business days ($12.99 flat rate).
*   **Curbside Pickup:** Free. Select your preferred store at checkout; your order will be ready for pickup within 2 hours of confirmation.

**What is the return window for Bluepeak purchases?**
We offer a 30-day return policy for most items. To be eligible for a full refund, items must be in original packaging with the receipt or digital order confirmation. Please note that "Clearance" items marked with a red tag are final sale and cannot be returned.

### Getting Help
If you encounter an issue not covered here, our support team is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST via the Live Chat feature in the app or by emailing support@bluepeakretail.com.
