# Bluepeak Retail Group: Organizational Structure and Reporting Protocols (Q3 2024)

**Document Owner:** Department of Human Resources  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2024  
**Review Date:** January 1, 2025

### Overview
This document outlines the official reporting hierarchy for Bluepeak Retail Group. Following the Q2 restructuring of the Omnichannel Integration team, these lines are now binding for all performance reviews, budgetary approvals, and operational escalations. All employees are expected to adhere to the chain of command to prevent cross-departmental friction and "skip-level" communication gaps.

### Executive Leadership & Core Reporting
The organizational flow originates with **Marcus Thorne (CEO)**. Direct reports to the CEO include:
*   **Sarah Jenkins (CFO):** Overseeing Corporate Finance, Tax, and Audit.
*   **David Chen (COO):** Managing Logistics, Supply Chain, and Store Operations.
*   **Elena Rodriguez (Chief Merchandising Officer):** Leading Procurement, Category Management, and Vendor Relations.

### Departmental Breakdown & Key Nodes
**1. Store Operations (Reporting to David Chen)**
The regional structure is divided into four territories. **Regional Managers (RMs)** are the primary points of contact for all Store Managers. 
*   *North East Region:* Managed by Julian Vance.
*   *South West Region:* Managed by Maya Gupta.
Any personnel grievances at the store level must first be vetted by the RM before being escalated to David Chen’s office.

**2. Omnichannel & Digital Strategy (Reporting to Elena Rodriguez)**
As of July 1, the E-commerce division has merged with Physical Merchandising. **Leo Sterling (VP of Digital Integration)** now reports directly to Elena Rodriguez. The UX/UI team and the Data Analytics wing have been moved under Leo’s purview to ensure a unified customer journey.

**3. Corporate Support Functions**
HR, Legal, and IT report via a dotted line to the CEO but maintain a solid reporting line to **Sarah Jenkins (CFO)** for budgetary oversight.

### Communication Protocols & Escalation
*   **Standard Reporting:** Daily operational updates should be delivered to the immediate supervisor via the weekly Sync Report.
*   **Urgent Escalations:** Matters involving legal liability or safety breaches must bypass standard lines and be reported immediately to the HR Director, **Claire Holloway**, and the Legal Counsel.
*   **Skip-Level Meetings:** While Bluepeak encourages transparency, employees wishing to meet with a supervisor’s manager must first notify their direct lead to ensure alignment on the agenda.

Failure to adhere to these reporting lines may result in administrative delays regarding payroll approvals and performance sign-offs.
