# Bluepeak Retail Group: Data Privacy FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 12, 2023

At Bluepeak Retail Group, we are committed to transparency regarding how we handle your personal information across our e-commerce platforms and physical storefronts. This FAQ outlines our data practices and your rights as a consumer.

### What specific information does Bluepeak collect?
We collect information necessary to fulfill your orders and improve your shopping experience. This includes:
*   **Identity Data:** Name, email address, and shipping/billing addresses.
*   **Transaction Data:** Order history, payment method (processed via secure encrypted tokens), and loyalty program status.
*   **Technical Data:** IP addresses and browser cookies used to remember your cart items and regional store preferences.

### How is my data stored and secured?
All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption standards. We utilize a multi-region cloud architecture to ensure redundancy and availability. Payment information is never stored on our local servers; we use PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (such as Stripe and PayPal) to handle all financial transactions.

### Who has access to my personal information?
Access is strictly limited to authorized personnel within the Support, Logistics, and Marketing departments who require the data to perform their job functions. We do not sell your personal identity data to third-party brokers. We share data with trusted partners only when necessary—for example, providing your address to FedEx or UPS for delivery.

### How can I request a copy of my data or ask for its deletion?
Under our Privacy Guarantee, you have the right to access and manage your information:
1.  **Data Access:** Log into your Bluepeak Account settings and select "Download My Data." You will receive a CSV file via email within 48 hours.
2.  **Deletion Requests:** To request the permanent deletion of your account and associated data, email `privacy@bluepeakretail.com` with the subject line "Data Erasure Request." Our compliance team will process this request within 30 business days.

### How long do you keep my information?
We retain transaction records for seven years to comply with federal tax and auditing regulations. Marketing preferences and loyalty profiles are maintained as long as your account remains active or until a deletion request is submitted.
