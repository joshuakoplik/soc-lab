# Order Cancellation & Return Policy FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023  
**Scope:** Internal Use Only (Employee Reference)

This document serves as the primary guidance for support agents handling customer requests to cancel orders across Bluepeak’s e-commerce and in-store pickup channels.

### When can an order be cancelled?
Orders can be cancelled instantly if they are still in the **"Pending"** or **"Processing"** status within the Order Management System (OMS). Once an order moves to **"Shipped"** or **"Out for Delivery,"** it can no longer be cancelled. In these cases, agents must instruct the customer to initiate a standard return once the package arrives.

### What is the "Golden Window"?
The Golden Window is the 60-minute period following order placement. During this time, customers can cancel via their account dashboard without agent intervention. After 60 minutes, orders enter the warehouse queue, and agents must manually verify if the shipment has been palletized before confirming a cancellation.

### How do I handle "Cancellation Requests" for Final Sale items?
Items marked as **Final Sale** (typically found in the Clearance section) cannot be cancelled once the payment has been captured. If a customer requests a cancellation for these items, refer them to the *Terms of Service* regarding non-refundable clearance goods. Exceptions may only be granted with approval from a Support Lead.

### Refund Timelines
Once a cancellation is processed in the OMS:
*   **Credit Card/PayPal:** Refunds are issued immediately but may take 3–5 business days to appear on the customer's statement depending on their bank.
*   **Bluepeak Store Credit:** These are applied to the loyalty account instantly and are available for use within 24 hours.

### Procedure for "Shipment in Transit" Requests
If a customer insists on cancelling an item that has already left the distribution center:
1. Do **not** mark the order as cancelled in the system.
2. Provide the customer with a pre-paid return shipping label via the Support Portal.
3. Advise them to refuse the delivery at the door if possible, which triggers an automatic return-to-sender process and speeds up the refund timeline by 48 hours.

### Escalation Path
If you encounter a system error (Error Code 402: Payment Lock) during the cancellation process, escalate the ticket immediately to the **Billing & Finance Team** via the #finance-ops Slack channel.
