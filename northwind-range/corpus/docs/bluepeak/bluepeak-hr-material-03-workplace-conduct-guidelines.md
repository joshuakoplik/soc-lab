# Bluepeak Retail Group: Workplace Conduct & Professionalism Guidelines

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Document Owner:** HR Department / People Operations  
**Scope:** All Corporate and Store-Level Employees

### Overview
At Bluepeak Retail Group, we strive to create an environment where creativity in retail strategy meets professional discipline. These guidelines ensure that our corporate offices and storefronts remain productive spaces centered on mutual respect and operational excellence.

### Professional Interactions & Communication
Bluepeak maintains a "Respect First" policy. We expect all employees—regardless of rank—to communicate with courtesy. 
*   **Internal Comms:** When using Slack or Microsoft Teams, keep discussions professional. Venting about store performance or management in public channels is prohibited; please use the "Open Door" portal for grievances.
*   **Conflict Resolution:** If a dispute arises between a Store Manager and a District Lead, the matter should be documented via an Incident Report form and submitted to HR within 48 hours.

### Attendance and Punctuality
Reliability is critical to our supply chain and customer experience.
*   **Corporate Staff:** Core hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Employees utilizing the "Flexible Friday" remote option must be available via Teams for the weekly Sync Meeting at 10:30 AM.
*   **Store Staff:** Shift adherence is mandatory. A "tardy" is defined as arriving more than seven minutes after a scheduled start time. Three unexcused tardies within a rolling 30-day period will trigger a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

### Workplace Environment & Dress Code
While we encourage individual expression, our image reflects the Bluepeak brand.
*   **Corporate Office:** We follow a "Business Casual" standard. Denim is permitted on Fridays provided it is neat and free of rips.
*   **Retail Floor:** Employees must wear the Bluepeak-issued navy polo or branded blouse. Footwear must be closed-toe for safety reasons; sneakers are acceptable if they are clean and neutral in color.

### Use of Company Technology
Company laptops and handheld inventory scanners are provided for business use only. 
*   **Personal Devices:** To ensure customer privacy and data security, personal mobile phone use is prohibited while on the sales floor or during active customer interactions. Phones should be stored in employee lockers during shifts.

Failure to adhere to these guidelines may result in disciplinary action, ranging from a written warning to termination, as outlined in the Employee Handbook. For questions, contact Sarah Jenkins in HR at extension 402.
