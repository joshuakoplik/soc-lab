# Employee Separation and Termination Protocol

**Document ID:** HR-POL-402  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources Department  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only

This document outlines the mandatory procedural requirements for the involuntary separation of employees within Bluepeak Retail Group. To mitigate legal risk and ensure brand consistency across our regional hubs, no termination may be executed without a formal sign-off from the Regional HR Director.

### 1. Pre-Termination Requirements
Before a termination meeting is scheduled, the Department Manager must submit a "Separation Request Packet" to HR. This packet must include:
*   **Performance Documentation:** At least two written warnings or a completed Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) dated within the last six months.
*   **Attendance Logs:** Time-clock data from the Kronos system verifying any patterns of absenteeism.
*   **Final Pay Calculation:** A request for final wages, including accrued PTO, to be processed by Payroll in accordance with state laws.

### 2. The Termination Meeting
Meetings must take place in a private office, ideally at the end of the business day to maintain the dignity of the employee and minimize disruption to store operations. An HR representative must act as the witness; the manager should lead the conversation but defer all benefit-related questions to HR.

### 3. Asset Recovery and Access Revocation
Immediate revocation of digital access is critical to protect proprietary vendor lists and pricing strategies. IT will disable Active Directory credentials exactly five minutes prior to the meeting start time. 

The following physical assets must be recovered before the employee leaves the premises:
*   Company-issued tablets/handhelds (Zebra scanners).
*   Store keys and security fobs.
*   Corporate credit cards.

### 4. Documentation Example
For auditing purposes, all termination files must contain a signed "Notice of Separation" form. A typical entry in the HRIS system for a processed termination should follow this format:
*   **Employee:** Marcus Thorne (ID #BP-8821)
*   **Contact:** m.thorne@email-provider.net | 555-012-9943
*   **Reason:** Repeated violation of Loss Prevention Policy (Section 4.2).
*   **Effective Date:** October 12, 2023.

### 5. Post-Separation Communication
Managers are prohibited from discussing the details of a termination with remaining staff. The approved messaging is: *"Marcus Thorne is no longer with Bluepeak Retail Group; we wish him well in his future endeavors."* Any inquiries regarding vacancy filling should be directed to the Talent Acquisition lead.
