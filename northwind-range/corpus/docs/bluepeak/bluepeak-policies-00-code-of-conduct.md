# Bluepeak Retail Group: Professional Standards & Code of Conduct

**Document Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Review Cycle:** Annual (Next review: Jan 2025)  
**Classification:** Internal / Confidential

### 1. Operational Integrity and Vendor Relations
Bluepeak employees must maintain a strict boundary between personal interests and corporate procurement. To prevent conflicts of interest within our supply chain, any employee with purchasing authority—specifically those operating within the Inventory Management or Store Operations teams—is prohibited from accepting gifts, entertainment, or gratuities exceeding $50 in fair market value from current or prospective vendors. 

All gifts must be logged via the *Vendor Disclosure Portal* within five business days of receipt. Failure to disclose items from key partners (e.g., Global Logistics Corp or Apex Textiles) will be treated as a breach of fiduciary duty and may result in immediate disciplinary action, up to termination.

### 2. Store-Level Conduct and Brand Representation
As the face of Bluepeak, floor staff and management are required to adhere to the "Client-First" interaction protocol. This includes the mandatory greeting of customers within ten seconds of entry and a strict prohibition on personal mobile device usage while on the sales floor.

Employee discounts (standardized at 20% for full-time staff) are for personal use only. Reselling merchandise purchased via employee discount on third-party platforms such as Poshmark or eBay is strictly forbidden. Audit teams conduct random spot-checks of transaction logs; any pattern suggestive of unauthorized reselling will trigger a formal HR review.

### 3. Data Privacy and Internal Communications
Confidentiality regarding our pricing strategies, seasonal promotional calendars, and regional sales targets is paramount. The distribution of internal "Weekly Performance Snapshots" to individuals outside the Bluepeak network is a violation of this code.

All internal communications must be conducted via company-approved channels (Microsoft Teams and Outlook). Use of unauthorized messaging apps for business decisions is prohibited to ensure compliance with our data retention policies.

### 4. Reporting and Compliance
Employees who witness violations of these standards are encouraged to report them directly to their Regional Manager or through the anonymous *Bluepeak Integrity Line*. The Admin department guarantees non-retaliation for reports made in good faith. All reported incidents will be investigated by the Administration and HR teams within ten business days of the filing date.
