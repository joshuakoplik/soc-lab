# Q3 Payroll Cost Analysis & Variance Summary

**Document Owner:** Finance Department  
**Date of Issue:** October 12, 2023  
**Reporting Period:** July 1, 2023 – September 30, 2023  
**Distribution:** Internal / All Management

### Executive Overview
The third quarter saw a moderate increase in total payroll expenditure compared to Q2. Total gross payroll costs for the period reached $4.2M, representing a 4.8% increase over the previous quarter. This uptick is primarily attributed to seasonal hiring surges across our Mid-Atlantic regional hubs and an adjustment in base pay for store managers implemented in August.

### Cost Breakdown by Category
*   **Base Salaries (Full-Time):** $2.1M. This remains the largest cost center, with a slight increase following the "Retail Leadership Retention" pay bump of 3% applied to all Store Managers and Assistant Managers.
*   **Hourly Wages (Part-Time/Seasonal):** $1.4M. We experienced a spike in September as we onboarded 45 additional seasonal associates ahead of the Q4 holiday rush.
*   **Overtime (OT) Pay:** $450,000. OT costs rose by 12% this quarter. Analysis indicates that the surge was concentrated in the logistics and warehousing division due to the integration of the new Inventory Management System (IMS), which required extended shifts for training and data migration.
*   **Benefits & Payroll Taxes:** $250,000. These costs remained stable and aligned with budget projections.

### Variance Analysis
The primary variance noted is a $110,000 overage in the Logistics department's labor budget. While this was unplanned, Finance has reclassified this as a "One-Time Implementation Cost" associated with the IMS rollout rather than an operational inefficiency. 

Conversely, the Retail Store Operations division came in 2% under budget due to higher-than-expected attrition in the Northeast region during July and August, resulting in lower aggregate hourly payouts before the September hiring wave.

### Q4 Outlook & Compliance
Looking ahead to October through December, we anticipate a further 15% increase in hourly wage costs. All department heads are reminded that overtime must be pre-approved by Regional Directors via the Workday portal to prevent budget slippage during the peak season. Payroll processing for the holiday bonus cycle will begin on November 15th; please ensure all performance reviews are submitted to HR by November 1st to avoid payment delays.
