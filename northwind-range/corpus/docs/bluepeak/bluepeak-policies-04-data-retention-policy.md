# Bluepeak Retail Group: Corporate Data Retention & Disposal Policy

**Policy Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

### 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the mandatory timeframes for the retention of physical and digital records at Bluepeak Retail Group. The goal is to ensure compliance with retail tax laws, labor regulations, and operational efficiency by preventing the indefinite storage of redundant data across our corporate headquarters and regional distribution centers.

### 2. Retention Schedules
Employees must adhere to the following retention periods based on document classification:

*   **Financial & Tax Records:** All payroll registers, quarterly VAT filings, and annual profit/loss statements must be retained for **seven (7) years**. This includes digital copies stored in the NetSuite ERP system and physical ledgers held at the corporate office.
*   **Employee Personnel Files:** Active employee records are maintained throughout their tenure. Upon termination of employment, files—including performance reviews and contracts—must be archived for **five (5) years** before being purged.
*   **Customer Transaction Data:** Point-of-Sale (POS) transaction logs and loyalty program activity are retained for **three (3) years** to facilitate returns and trend analysis. After this period, personally identifiable information (PII) must be anonymized or deleted.
*   **Vendor Contracts & Leases:** Signed agreements with suppliers and commercial real estate leases must be kept for the **duration of the contract plus four (4) years** following expiration.

### 3. Disposal Procedures
Data must not be deleted haphazardly. The following disposal protocols are mandatory:

*   **Physical Documents:** All paper records containing sensitive information (e.g., employee SSNs, vendor bank details) must be disposed of via the secure shredding bins provided by Shred-It in each department. Do not use standard waste bins.
*   **Digital Data:** The IT Department will perform a global "Data Scrub" on the first Saturday of every July. Department heads must notify the Admin Department by June 15th if any specific datasets are subject to a "Legal Hold" due to ongoing litigation or audits, which overrides standard deletion cycles.

### 4. Compliance
Failure to adhere to these retention timelines may result in excessive storage costs and legal risks. The Administration Department will conduct random spot-checks of regional filing cabinets twice per year to ensure legacy documents are being purged according to this schedule.
