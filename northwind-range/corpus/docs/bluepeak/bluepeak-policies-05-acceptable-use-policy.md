# Bluepeak Retail Group: IT & Systems Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

### 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the standards for using Bluepeak Retail Group’s digital assets. It applies to all full-time employees, seasonal contractors, and third-party vendors accessing our corporate network, Point-of-Sale (POS) systems, and handheld inventory scanners.

### 2. Hardware and Asset Management
All hardware issued by the Admin Department—including Dell Latitude laptops, iPads for floor management, and Zebra scanners—remains the sole property of Bluepeak Retail Group. 
*   **Physical Security:** Laptops must be locked in secure office drawers when not in use at store locations.
*   **Modifications:** Employees may not install unauthorized hardware peripherals (e.g., personal USB hubs or external drives) into POS terminals to prevent malware injection.

### 3. Software and Network Usage
The corporate network is provided primarily for business operations. While incidental personal use is permitted during designated break periods, the following are strictly prohibited:
*   **Unauthorized Software:** Installation of non-approved software (e.g., torrent clients, unauthorized VPNs, or cryptocurrency miners) on company machines. 
*   **Cloud Storage:** Use of personal Dropbox or Google Drive accounts for storing Bluepeak proprietary data; all files must be saved to the corporate SharePoint directory.
*   **Bandwidth:** Streaming high-definition video or gaming during shift hours, which may degrade POS transaction speeds for customers.

### 4. Data Security and Privacy
Protecting customer PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is our highest priority.
*   **Password Integrity:** Passwords must be changed every 90 days via the Bluepeak Identity Portal. Sharing login credentials for the "Bluepeak-Admin" or "Store-Manager" accounts is a Tier-1 policy violation.
*   **Email Conduct:** Employees must not use their @bluepeakretail.com email address to register for non-work related newsletters or retail discount clubs.

### 5. Compliance and Enforcement
The Admin Department, in coordination with IT Security, conducts random audits of system logs. Violations of this policy will be documented and forwarded to Human Resources. Depending on the severity—particularly regarding data breaches or unauthorized software installation—disciplinary actions may range from a formal written warning to immediate termination of employment.
