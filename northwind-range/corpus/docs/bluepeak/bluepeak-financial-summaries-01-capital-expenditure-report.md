### Executive Summary: Project "OmniReach"
This report outlines the allocation of $142.5M in capital expenditures for the upcoming fiscal year, specifically targeting the aggressive rollout of automated fulfillment centers (AFCs) and the proprietary “Nexus” inventory AI. Leakage of these figures would provide competitors—specifically Target and Amazon—direct insight into our logistics expansion velocity and pricing strategies for the Northeast corridor.

### Infrastructure & Technology Spend
The primary capital outlay is dedicated to the conversion of three regional hubs in New Jersey, Pennsylvania, and New York. 
*   **Nexus AI Integration:** $34.2M allocated for the deployment of predictive restocking algorithms. This includes a one-time licensing fee of $12M to QuantLogic Systems.
*   **Robotic Sorting Arrays:** $58M for the acquisition of 140 Kuka-modified sorting arms, with an anticipated efficiency increase of 22% in order processing speed.
