# Bluepeak Retail Group: Compensation Bands Overview

**Document Owner:** Department of Human Resources  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Review Cycle:** Annual (Q4)

### Purpose and Philosophy
Bluepeak Retail Group utilizes a structured compensation band system to ensure internal equity, market competitiveness, and transparent career progression. Our philosophy is to reward both tenure and performance while aligning our payroll with the current retail landscape across our North American operations. Each role is assigned a Grade Level based on the complexity of responsibilities, required certifications, and impact on revenue.

### Compensation Structure
Compensation is divided into five primary bands. Each band consists of a minimum, a midpoint (the market rate), and a maximum. Movement within a band occurs via annual merit increases or promotional adjustments.

| Grade | Job Family Example | Min Salary | Midpoint | Max Salary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade 1** | Store Associate / Warehouse Clerk | $32,000 | $38,500 | $44,000 |
| **Grade 2** | Shift Lead / Inventory Coordinator | $42,000 | $51,000 | $60,000 |
| **Grade 3** | Store Manager / Regional Buyer | $62,000 | $78,000 | $94,000 |
| **Grade 4** | District Manager / HR Specialist | $85,000 | $105,000 | $125,000 |
| **Grade 5** | Director of Operations / VP Level | $130,000 | $165,000 | $200,000 |

### Progression and Adjustments
Employees are typically hired at the minimum or slightly above based on proven experience. Merit increases are calculated as a percentage of the current salary but cannot push an employee beyond the maximum of their current band. To move to a higher band (e.g., Grade 2 to Grade 3), an employee must undergo a formal promotion process, which includes a performance review by their direct supervisor and approval from the Regional Director.

### Inquiry Process
For questions regarding your specific placement within a band or to request a salary review based on new certifications, please submit a ticket via the HR Portal. 

**Example Submission:**
To ensure requests are tracked, please use the following format: *Employee Name | Employee ID | Current Grade.*
*Example: Sarah Jenkins | EMP-88219 | Grade 2 (s.jenkins@bluepeakretail.com)*

All compensation inquiries will be responded to within five business days by the assigned HR Business Partner for your region.
