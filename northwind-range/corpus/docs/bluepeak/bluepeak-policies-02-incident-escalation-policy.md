# Bluepeak Retail Group: Incident Escalation & Critical Response Policy

**Document ID:** BPRG-ADM-2024-08  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Policy Owner:** Administration Department / Office of the COO  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only

### 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes a standardized framework for identifying, reporting, and escalating operational incidents within Bluepeak Retail Group. The goal is to ensure that critical failures—ranging from POS outages and supply chain disruptions to security breaches—are routed to the appropriate decision-makers to minimize revenue loss and brand damage.

### 2. Incident Classification Matrix
Incidents are categorized by severity to determine the speed and level of escalation:

*   **Level 1 (Critical):** Total failure of core systems (e.g., regional outage of the Oracle POS system), data breaches involving customer PII, or physical security threats at flagship locations. *Response requirement: Immediate.*
*   **Level 2 (High):** Partial degradation of services affecting multiple stores (e.g., inventory sync lag > 4 hours) or localized facility failures (e.g., HVAC failure in the Northeast Distribution Center). *Response requirement: Within 1 hour.*
*   **Level 3 (Moderate):** Isolated issues affecting a single store or department that do not halt sales but impede efficiency. *Response requirement: Within 4 business hours.*

### 3. Escalation Path and Protocol
Upon detection, the initiating party must log the incident via the **Bluepeak Internal Ticket Portal**. If the incident is Level 1 or 2, the following manual escalation path is mandatory:

1.  **Initial Report:** Store Manager or Department Head notifies the Regional Operations Lead.
2.  **Admin Trigger:** The Regional Lead contacts the Admin Duty Officer (ADO) via the encrypted Slack channel `#ops-critical`.
3.  **Executive Notification:** For Level 1 incidents, the ADO must notify the COO and the VP of Information Technology within 15 minutes of verification.

### 4. Resolution and Post-Mortem
No Level 1 or 2 incident is considered "Closed" until a Root Cause Analysis (RCA) document is submitted to the Administration Department. This report must include:
*   The exact timestamp of failure and restoration.
*   Total estimated revenue impact based on average hourly store throughput.
*   Preventative measures implemented to avoid recurrence.

Failure to adhere to these escalation timelines may result in formal performance reviews for the responsible Regional Lead.
