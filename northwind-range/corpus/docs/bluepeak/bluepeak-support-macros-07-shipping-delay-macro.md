# Shipping Delay Response Macros: Logistics & Fulfillment

**Document Owner:** Support Department / Customer Experience Team  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023  
**Scope:** Internal Use Only

This document contains approved response templates for handling shipping delays. To maintain brand consistency and manage customer expectations, please use these macros based on the specific cause of the delay identified in the Order Management System (OMS).

### Macro 1: General Logistics Delay (Carrier Side)
*Use this when the package has left the Bluepeak warehouse but is stalled with FedEx or UPS.*

"Thank you for reaching out about your order. I have checked the tracking details, and it appears your package is currently experiencing a transit delay at the Memphis hub. While we don’t have direct control over carrier movements, we are monitoring the status closely. If there is no movement on your tracking link within the next 48 hours, please let us know and we will initiate a trace request with the carrier to locate your items."

### Macro 2: Warehouse Backlog (Internal Delay)
*Use this when the order is still in 'Processing' status due to high volume at our East Coast Distribution Center.*

"I apologize for the delay in getting your order shipped. Due to an unexpected surge in demand following our Autumn Collection launch, our fulfillment center is currently running 3-5 business days behind schedule. Your order is confirmed and is being prepared for shipment. As a gesture of goodwill for the wait, I have applied a $10 credit to your Bluepeak loyalty account for use on a future purchase."

### Macro 3: Out-of-Stock/Inventory Error
*Use this when an item was sold but is actually unavailable in the warehouse.*

"I am writing to apologize for a mistake regarding the availability of the item in your order. Due to an inventory synchronization error, we are currently out of stock of this specific product. We expect our next shipment from the supplier on November 2nd. You can choose to keep the order active and receive it as soon as it arrives, or I can issue a full refund to your original payment method immediately. Please let me know which you prefer."

### Escalation Procedure
If a customer is requesting an expedited shipping upgrade due to these delays, Support Leads are authorized to upgrade Shipping to 'Overnight' at no cost if the delay exceeds 7 business days. Please tag these tickets with `#LogisticsEscalation` for weekly reporting to the Operations team.
