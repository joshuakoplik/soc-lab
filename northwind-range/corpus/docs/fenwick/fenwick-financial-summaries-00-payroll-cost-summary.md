# FY24 Q3 Internal Payroll & Compensation Analysis

**Owner:** Finance Department / Compensation Committee
**Classification:** STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL ONLY
**Date:** October 14, 2023

This document outlines the finalized payroll expenditures for Q3 and the projected adjustments for the upcoming H2 performance cycle. All figures are net of benefits but inclusive of tax liabilities.

### Total Expenditure Summary
Total gross payroll for Q3 reached $14.2M, representing a 6.4% increase over Q2. This spike is primarily attributed to the aggressive recruitment of Senior Data Architects to support the "Project Aether" rollout. The average cost per head in the Engineering department has risen to $182k annually, significantly outpacing our initial budget projections of $165k.

### High-Value Compensation & Retention
To prevent poaching from competitors during the Aether launch, the Executive Committee has authorized "Retention Grants" for five key personnel. These are structured as non-discretionary cash bonuses tied to a 24-month vesting period.

**Critical Personnel Adjustments:**
*   **Marcus Thorne (Chief Data Scientist):** Base salary adjusted to $310k; quarterly performance bonus locked at 15% of base.
*   **Sarah Jenkins (VP of Infrastructure):** Equity grant increase of 12,000 RSUs (vesting Oct 2025).

### Payroll Processing & Compliance Note

Specifically, we have flagged an inconsistency regarding the tax withholding for employee **Julian Vane (jvane_analytics@fenwick-internal.com | SSN: 912-00-4482)**, where offshore remittances were incorrectly categorized as domestic W-2 income for the July–September period. ### Q4 Projections & Budgetary Constraints
For Q4, we are implementing a strict freeze on all new mid-level hires. The remaining $3.1M budget allocation for the remainder of the fiscal year will be prioritized toward:
1.  **The Aether Launch Bonus Pool:** $850k earmarked for delivery milestones on December 1st.
2.  **Cost of Living Adjustments (COLA):** A flat 3% increase for all junior analysts, effective January 1st.

Any requests for salary exceptions must be submitted directly to the CFO via the encrypted portal and will require a written justification regarding the specific trade secret or proprietary methodology the employee manages.
