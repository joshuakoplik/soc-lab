# Fenwick Analytics: Flexible Work & Remote Operations Policy

**Policy Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Scope:** All full-time and contract employees

### Overview
Fenwick Analytics supports a hybrid operational model to balance individual flexibility with the collaborative needs of our data science and engineering teams. This policy outlines the requirements for maintaining productivity and security while working outside the primary office hubs.

### Work Modalities
Employees are categorized into one of three work designations, determined by their Department Head:
1. **Office-First:** Required on-site Monday through Thursday.
2. **Hybrid:** Required on-site Tuesdays and Wednesdays (Core Collaboration Days) to facilitate sprint planning and cross-functional syncs.
3. **Remote-First:** Eligible for 100% remote work, provided they reside within a timezone no more than three hours offset from Eastern Standard Time (EST).

### Core Hours & Availability
Regardless of location, all staff must be online and reachable via Slack and Zoom during the company’s core window: **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST**. Meetings should be scheduled within this window to avoid infringing on personal time across different regions. Status updates must be posted in the `#daily-standup` channel by 10:30 AM EST.

### Equipment and Stipends
Fenwick Analytics provides a standard hardware bundle (MacBook Pro, dual monitors, and peripherals). For those designated as Hybrid or Remote-First, a one-time home-office setup stipend of $500 is available upon hire. 

Monthly internet subsidies of $60 are processed via the Expensify portal on the last Friday of each month; employees must upload a redacted copy of their service bill to qualify.

### Security and Compliance
Given the sensitivity of our client datasets, remote work is subject to the following mandates:
* **VPN Usage:** All connections to the Fenwick Data Lake must be routed through the GlobalProtect VPN with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) enabled.
* **Hardware:** Work must be performed exclusively on company-issued hardware. Use of personal laptops for client data analysis is strictly prohibited.
* **Environment:** Employees are expected to maintain a professional environment during client-facing calls, ensuring a neutral background and minimal noise interference.

### Performance Tracking
Remote performance is measured by output rather than activity. Managers will utilize Jira velocity charts and monthly OKR reviews to ensure deliverables are met. Failure to attend Core Collaboration Days without prior approval from Admin may result in a reclassification to "Office-First" status.
