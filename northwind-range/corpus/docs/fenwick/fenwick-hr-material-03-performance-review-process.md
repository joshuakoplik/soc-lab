# Fenwick Analytics: Bi-Annual Performance & Compensation Calibration Process

**Document Owner:** Human Resources Department  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Internal / Confidential

### Overview
Fenwick Analytics utilizes a synchronized bi-annual review cycle to align individual technical contributions with our quarterly growth targets. The goal of this process is to move away from static annual reviews toward a dynamic "Calibration Model" that accounts for the high volatility of data analytics project timelines.

### Timeline and Milestones
The performance cycle occurs in two primary windows: **H1 (January–June)** and **H2 (July–December)**. 

*   **Self-Assessment Window:** Open from the 1st to the 15th of the final month of each half. Employees must submit their self-evaluations via the Workday portal, specifically highlighting KPIs related to billable utilization rates and codebase efficiency.
*   **Managerial Review:** From the 16th to the 30th, Department Heads (Engineering, Data Science, and Client Strategy) will draft evaluations.
*   **Calibration Committee:** During the first two weeks of the new half, HR and Executive Leadership meet to normalize ratings across teams to prevent "rating inflation" within specific pods.

### The Rating Scale & Compensation Linkage
Fenwick employs a four-tier rating system. These ratings directly dictate the Merit Increase Budget (MIB) allocated to each department:

1.  **Exceeds Expectations (EE):** Eligible for top-tier bonus multipliers and priority consideration for "Senior" or "Principal" title promotions.
2.  **Meets Expectations (ME):** Standard cost-of-living adjustments and performance bonuses aligned with the base salary band.
3.  **Developing (DEV):** Indicates a gap in technical proficiency or soft skills. Requires a mandatory 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) focusing on specific certifications or project milestones.
4.  **Needs Immediate Action (NIA):** Immediate review for termination or role reassignment.

### Specific Evaluation Criteria
Unlike generic reviews, Fenwick evaluators must score employees on the following proprietary metrics:
*   **The Accuracy Quotient:** The delta between predicted model outcomes and actual client results.
*   **Cross-Functional Velocity:** Ability to transition data pipelines from the Engineering team to the Client Strategy team without significant rework.
*   **Client Retention Impact:** Direct correlation between the employee’s deliverables and the renewal of annual service contracts.

Failure to adhere to these deadlines or the objective scoring criteria may result in a delay of bonus payouts for the entire reporting line.
