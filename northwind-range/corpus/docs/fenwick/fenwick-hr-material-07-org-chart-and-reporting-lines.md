# Fenwick Analytics: Organizational Structure and Reporting Protocols

**Document ID:** HR-OP-2024-08  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2023  
**Owner:** People Operations (HR)  
**Classification:** Confidential / Internal Only

### Overview
This document outlines the current reporting hierarchy for Fenwick Analytics. To maintain agility across our data engineering and client delivery streams, we utilize a matrix management model. While employees have a primary functional manager for career development, they may report to different Project Leads based on specific client engagements (e.g., the CURRENT-GEN healthcare migration).

### Executive Leadership & Direct Reports
The organizational head is **Marcus Thorne (CEO)**. The following executive team reports directly to the CEO:
*   **Sarah Jenkins (COO):** Oversees Global Delivery, Infrastructure, and HR.
*   **David Chen (CTO):** Oversees Product Engineering, Data Science R&D, and Security.
*   **Elena Rodriguez (CFO):** Oversees Finance, Legal, and Procurement.

### Functional Reporting Lines
#### 1. Data Engineering & Cloud Ops (Reporting to CTO)
The engineering wing is divided into three specialized pods. All Pod Leads report to **Julian Voss (VP of Engineering)**:
*   **ETL/Pipeline Pod:** Led by Maya Okoro. Focuses on ingestion frameworks.
*   **Warehouse Architecture Pod:** Led by Simon Glass. Focuses on Snowflake/BigQuery optimization.
*   **DevOps/SRE Pod:** Led by Tariq Aziz. Manages the Fenwick proprietary deployment engine.

#### 2. Client Delivery & Analytics (Reporting to COO)
Delivery is managed via a regional structure. Regional Directors report to **Sarah Jenkins**:
*   **North America (NAMER):** Led by Chloe Whitmore.
*   **EMEA:** Led by Alistair Finch.
*   **APAC:** Led by Mei Lin.

### Reporting Protocols & Escalations
To prevent bottlenecks, Fenwick adheres to the "Two-Level Rule." Employees are encouraged to resolve operational blockers with their immediate supervisor first. If a resolution is not reached within 48 business hours, the issue should be escalated to the next level of management (e.g., Pod Lead $\rightarrow$ VP of Engineering).

**Cross-Functional Conflict:** In instances where a functional manager and a project lead provide conflicting directives, the Project Lead holds primary authority over deliverables, while the Functional Manager retains authority over performance reviews and professional development.

### Update Cycle
This chart is audited quarterly. Any changes to reporting lines resulting from promotions or restructuring must be submitted via the *Workday Change Request* portal and approved by HR before being publicized internally.
