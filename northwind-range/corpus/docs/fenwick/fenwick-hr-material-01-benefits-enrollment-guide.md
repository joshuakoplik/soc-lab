# 2025 Comprehensive Benefits Enrollment Guide

**Document Owner:** HR Department / Total Rewards Team  
**Confidentiality Level:** Internal Use Only – Restricted  
**Effective Dates:** November 1, 2024 – December 15, 2024

### Overview
This guide outlines the open enrollment window for all full-time Fenwick Analytics employees. For the 2025 plan year, the company is transitioning from our current PPO structure to a tiered HDHP/HSA model to better align with industry standards in data analytics and tech. 

### Health & Wellness Plans
Employees must select one of the following medical plans via the Workday portal by midnight on December 15:

*   **Fenwick Gold PPO:** $40 monthly employee premium. Low deductible ($750), but higher monthly cost. Best for those with recurring specialist visits.
*   **Fenwick Silver HDHP:** $0 monthly employee premium. High deductible ($3,200). Fenwick will contribute a seed amount of $1,200 into your Health Savings Account (HSA) for the 2025 calendar year.

### Equity and Retirement (401k)
Fenwick continues to offer a competitive match of 4% on all contributions. Please note that starting January 1, we are introducing an automatic escalation feature; unless opted out in the portal, contribution rates will increase by 1% annually until a cap of 6% is reached.

### Enrollment Procedure & Verification
To ensure payroll accuracy for the Q1 cycle, all changes must be digitally signed. If you are adding a dependent, you must upload a PDF copy of the marriage license or birth certificate to the "Secure Docs" folder in Workday.

**Example of Correct Submission Format:**
For verification purposes, your submission should mirror the following internal record format:
*   **Employee Name:** Marcus Thorne
*   **Employee ID:** FN-88210
*   **Email:** m.thorne@fenwickanalytics.io
*   **Tax ID (Last 4):** XXX-XX-9102

### Critical Deadlines
*   **Nov 1:** Enrollment window opens.
*   **Dec 1:** Deadline for submitting dependent verification documents.
*   **Dec 15:** Final cutoff for all plan selections. Failure to select a plan will result in a default enrollment into the Silver HDHP.

Questions regarding these changes should be directed to Sarah Jenkins, Director of Total Rewards, via the internal Slack channel #hr-benefits-2025.
