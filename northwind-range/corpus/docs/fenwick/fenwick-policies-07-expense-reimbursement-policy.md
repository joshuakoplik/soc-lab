# Fenwick Analytics: Employee Expense & Reimbursement Policy

**Policy Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Updated:** November 12, 2023  
**Classification:** Internal / Confidential

### 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the standards for incurring and reclaiming business-related expenses. The objective is to ensure that Fenwick Analytics maintains fiscal discipline while providing employees with the necessary resources to perform their duties across our regional hubs. This policy applies to all full-time and contract staff.

### 2. Reimbursable Categories
**2.1 Travel & Lodging**  
Airfare must be booked through the *Navan* platform in Economy class. Business Class is only permissible for continuous flights exceeding eight hours, subject to VP-level approval. Hotel stays are capped at $275/night in Tier 1 cities (NYC, SF, London) and $180/night elsewhere.

**2.2 Client Entertainment & Meals**  
Fenwick prioritizes relationship management; however, meal expenses must not exceed $75 per person for client dinners. Internal team meals are capped at $30 per head and require a specific project code (e.g., PROJECT-DELTA) to be billed against the correct budget.

**2.3 Software & Hardware**  
Standard workstations are provided by IT. Any specialized analytics software or cloud computing credits (AWS/Azure) outside of the corporate master account must be pre-approved via a Jira ticket submitted to the Admin and IT departments before purchase.

### 3. Submission Procedure
All claims must be submitted through *Expensify* within 30 calendar days of the expenditure. Claims submitted after 60 days will be automatically rejected by the system and will require a written waiver from the CFO for manual override.

**Required Documentation:**  
*   Itemized receipts are mandatory for all transactions over $25. Credit card statements are not acceptable substitutes for itemized receipts.
*   For client meals, the names of all attendees and the business purpose must be noted in the "Comments" field.

### 4. Approval Workflow
1.  **Direct Manager:** Reviews for alignment with project budgets.
2.  **Admin Department:** Audits for policy compliance and tax documentation.
3.  **Finance:** Final disbursement via payroll on the second Friday of each month.

Failure to adhere to these guidelines may result in delayed reimbursement or disciplinary action in accordance with the Fenwick Employee Handbook.
