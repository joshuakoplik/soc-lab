# Q3 Departmental Budget Variance Report: FY24

**Owner:** Finance Department  
**Date:** October 14, 2024  
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL ONLY

### Executive Summary
The third quarter concluded with a total departmental spend of $4.2M against a budgeted allocation of $3.95M, representing an overall unfavorable variance of 6.3%. While the Professional Services arm remains under budget due to delayed onboarding, significant overages in Cloud Infrastructure and Talent Acquisition have pressured our quarterly margins.

### Variance Analysis by Department

**1. Data Engineering & Infrastructure (DEI)**
*   **Actual:** $1.82M | **Budget:** $1.50M | **Variance:** ($320k)
*   **Driver:** The primary driver is the unplanned scaling of our Snowflake warehouse instances to support the new "OmniView" client pilot. Data egress costs spiked in August following the migration of the legacy Teradata sets for the Global Logistics account. Additionally, a failure to negotiate the renewal of the Datadog contract prior to September 1st resulted in a 12% premium on the annual license.

**2. Product Development (PD)**
*   **Actual:** $940k | **Budget:** $1.1M | **Variance:** +$160k
*   **Driver:** The positive variance is attributed to the decision by Sarah Jenkins to defer the hire of two Senior Backend Engineers until Q4. This payroll saving offset a one-time $45k expenditure on specialized GPU compute rentals for the ML model training phase in July.

**3. Client Delivery & Professional Services (PS)**
*   **Actual:** $810k | **Budget:** $950k | **Variance:** +$140k
*   **Driver:** Under-spend is linked to a 20% slippage in the start dates for the Zenith Healthcare implementation. While this improves short-term cash flow, it creates a significant revenue recognition risk for Q4.

**4. General & Administrative (G&A)**
*   **Actual:** $630k | **Budget:** $400k | **Variance:** ($230k)
*   **Driver:** Unbudgeted legal fees associated with the intellectual property audit for the pending acquisition of NexaStream. Recruitment agency fees also exceeded projections due to the aggressive headhunting required for the Chief Data Officer role.

### Corrective Actions & Outlook
Effective immediately, all Cloud Infrastructure spend exceeding $15k per month requires a sign-off from the CFO’s office. The Finance team will meet with Sarah Jenkins on October 20th to realign the Product Development hiring roadmap against current cash flow constraints. We anticipate Q4 variances to stabilize as the OmniView pilot moves into a steady state of consumption.
