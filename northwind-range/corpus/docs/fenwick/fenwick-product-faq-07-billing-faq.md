# Fenwick Analytics Billing & Subscription FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This guide provides answers to common questions regarding billing cycles, plan adjustments, and payment methods for the Fenwick Analytics platform.

### General Billing Questions

**What are your available pricing tiers?**  
We offer three primary tiers: **Starter** ($49/month), **Professional** ($199/month), and **Enterprise**. The Professional tier is our most popular option, offering expanded API call limits and priority support. Enterprise pricing is customized based on data volume and seat count; please contact your account manager for a quote.

**When am I billed each month?**  
Billing occurs on the anniversary date of your initial sign-up. For example, if you activated your Professional plan on the 12th of the month, your invoice will be generated and charged automatically on the 12th of every subsequent month.

**Which payment methods do you accept?**  
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and ACH transfers for Enterprise clients. We do not currently accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

### Plan Changes & Adjustments

**What happens if I upgrade my plan mid-cycle?**  
Upgrades are applied immediately. You will be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing period based on the difference between your old and new plans. Your next full invoice will reflect the new tier price.

**Can I downgrade my plan?**  
Yes. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle to ensure you retain access to your higher-tier features for the duration of the period you have already paid for. To initiate a downgrade, navigate to *Settings > Billing > Change Plan*.

**How are "Overage Credits" handled?**  
For users on the Starter and Professional plans, exceeding your monthly data processing limit triggers an automatic overage charge of $0.15 per GB. These charges are added to the following month's invoice under the line item "Data Overage."

### Invoices & Taxes

**Where can I find my past invoices?**  
Logged-in administrators can download PDF copies of all historical invoices by visiting the *Billing Portal* and selecting the *Invoice History* tab.

**How do I update my tax ID or VAT number?**  
To ensure correct tax application, please email `billing@fenwickanalytics.com` with your updated documentation. Changes will be reflected on the next invoice generated after the update is processed by our finance team.
