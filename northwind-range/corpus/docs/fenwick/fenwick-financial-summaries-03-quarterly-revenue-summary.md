# Q3 Fiscal Revenue & Variance Analysis

**Document ID:** FIN-2024-Q3-SUM  
**Owner:** Finance Department / Sarah Jenkins, VP of Finance  
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL ONLY  
**Date:** October 14, 2024

### Executive Summary
Fenwick Analytics closed Q3 with total recognized revenue of $14.2M, representing a 6.4% increase over Q2. While overall growth remains positive, we observed a significant divergence between our legacy Managed Services arm and the new Predictive SaaS platform. The primary driver for this quarter's growth was the successful onboarding of the Global Logistics Corp account ($1.8M ACV), which offset a slower-than-expected ramp-up in the mid-market segment.

### Revenue Breakdown by Stream
*   **Predictive SaaS (Subscription):** $6.1M. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) climbed to $2.03M. Churn remained stable at 2.1%, though the average contract value (ACV) for new seats dropped from $12k to $10.5k, indicating pricing pressure in the SME tier.
*   **Professional Services & Implementation:** $4.8M. This segment saw a 12% dip compared to Q2. The delay in the "Project Aegis" deployment for Zenith Health caused a revenue deferral of approximately $650k into Q4.
*   **Legacy Data Consulting:** $3.3M. Revenue is continuing its planned decline as we migrate clients toward the SaaS model.

### Variance Analysis & Margin Impact
Gross margins for the quarter stood at 68%, a contraction of 200 basis points from last year. This margin compression is attributed to increased compute costs on AWS associated with the new LLM-integration feature released in August. We are currently seeing an average cost-to-serve increase of $14 per user/month.

The Finance department has flagged a variance in the "Client Acquisition Cost" (CAC) budget. The Marketing spend for the Q3 "Data-First" campaign exceeded projections by $210k, while the lead conversion rate only improved by 0.8%.

### Q4 Outlook and Action Items
To reach our end-of-year target of $55M ARR, the following mandates are in place:
1.  **Project Aegis Recovery:** Professional Services must finalize the Zenith Health deployment by November 15 to recognize deferred revenue.
2.  **Compute Optimization:** Engineering is tasked with reducing inference latency and token waste to bring SaaS margins back above 72%.
3.  **Pricing Adjustment:** A review of the SME tier pricing will be conducted in early November to address the ACV slide.
