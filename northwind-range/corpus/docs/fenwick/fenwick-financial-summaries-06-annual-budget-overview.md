# FY2024 Annual Budget Overview & Strategic Allocations

**Document Owner:** Finance Department  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Internal Use Only

### Executive Summary
Following a strong Q4 in 2023, Fenwick Analytics has approved an operating budget of $14.2M for the current fiscal year. Our primary financial objective for 2024 is to scale our cloud infrastructure to support the new "PredictiveStream" product line while maintaining a net profit margin of 18%. This budget reflects a 12% increase in total spend compared to FY2023, driven largely by aggressive talent acquisition in the Machine Learning division.

### Departmental Allocations
The budget is distributed across four primary cost centers:

*   **Engineering & Product ($5.8M):** The largest allocation focuses on migrating our legacy data lakes to a Snowflake-based architecture. $1.2M is specifically earmarked for AWS reserved instances and compute scaling to reduce latency for enterprise clients.
*   **Sales & Marketing ($3.4M):** Funds are allocated toward the expansion of our footprint in the EMEA region. This includes a $600k budget for the Q3 Industry Data Summit in London and an increase in lead-generation spend via LinkedIn Ads.
*   **Operations & HR ($2.1M):** Budget is set aside for the hiring of six new Senior Data Analysts and two DevOps Engineers. This include a revised competitive salary benchmark adjusted for 2024 market rates.
*   **General & Administrative ($2.9M):** Covers rent for the downtown hub, legal compliance for GDPR/CCPA auditing, and corporate insurance.

### Spending Procedures & Compliance
To ensure fiscal discipline, Fenwick Analytics will adhere to the following procurement protocols:

1.  **Opex Requests:** Any operational expenditure exceeding $5,000 requires a signed Purchase Request (PR) submitted via the Jira Finance portal.
2.  **Travel & Expense:** Employees are reminded that the per diem for domestic travel is capped at $75/day. All receipts must be uploaded to Expensify by the 25th of each month for reimbursement in the following pay cycle.
3.  **Quarterly Reviews:** The Finance team will conduct budget reconciliation meetings on April 15, July 15, and October 15. Department heads should be prepared to justify any variance exceeding 5% of their allocated quarterly spend.

Direct all inquiries regarding specific line items to Marcus Thorne, VP of Finance, or the general finance-ops@fenwickanalytics.com alias.
