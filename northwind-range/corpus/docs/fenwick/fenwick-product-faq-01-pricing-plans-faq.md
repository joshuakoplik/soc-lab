# Fenwick Analytics Pricing Plans FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This document provides clarity on our current subscription tiers and billing procedures to help you choose the right plan for your data volume and analytical needs.

### Which plan is right for my organization?
Fenwick Analytics offers three primary tiers:
*   **Starter ($49/mo):** Ideal for freelancers or small teams. Includes 5GB of monthly data ingestion, basic SQL querying, and 3 user seats.
*   **Professional ($249/mo):** Designed for scaling businesses. Includes 50GB of monthly data ingestion, access to our Predictive Modeling engine, and up to 15 user seats.
*   **Enterprise (Custom):** For large-scale operations requiring unlimited ingestion, dedicated account management, and SSO integration. Please contact sales for a tailored quote.

### How is "Data Ingestion" measured?
Data ingestion refers to the total volume of raw data piped into Fenwick from your external sources (e.g., AWS S3, Google BigQuery, or Shopify API) per calendar month. We measure this by the compressed size of the payload. If you exceed your plan’s limit, you will be notified via email at 80% and 100% usage.

### What happens if I exceed my monthly data limit?
To prevent service interruptions, we apply "Burst Capacity." Once you hit your limit, you can continue ingesting data at a rate of $0.15 per additional GB for the remainder of the billing cycle. This ensures your dashboards remain live while you decide whether to upgrade your tier.

### Can I change my plan mid-cycle?
Yes. You can upgrade your plan instantly through the Billing Portal in your Dashboard. If you upgrade, the price difference will be prorated based on the remaining days in your current cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

### Are there discounts for annual commitments?
Yes. Customers who opt for annual billing receive a 15% discount compared to the monthly rate. This is applied automatically at checkout when "Annual" is selected.

### What is your refund policy for subscription fees?
We offer a full refund for any subscription started within the last 14 days, provided that less than 1GB of data has been ingested. To request a refund, please open a ticket with the Support team via the Help Center.
