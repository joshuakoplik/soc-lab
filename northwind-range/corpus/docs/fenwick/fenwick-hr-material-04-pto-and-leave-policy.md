# Fenwick Analytics: Comprehensive PTO and Leave Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-2024-08  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations Department  
**Classification:** Internal / Confidential

### 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
Fenwick Analytics utilizes a unified PTO bank combining vacation and personal days to provide maximum flexibility for our consultants and analysts. Accrual is based on tenure with the firm:

*   **Tier 1 (0–2 years):** Accrue 15 days per annum (1.25 days/month).
*   **Tier 2 (3–5 years):** Accrue 20 days per annum (1.67 days/month).
*   **Tier 3 (6+ years):** Accrue 25 days per annum (2.08 days/month).

PTO is credited to the employee's balance on the first business day of each month. A maximum carry-over of 40 hours is permitted into the following calendar year; any excess hours will be forfeited on December 31st to mitigate financial liability on the company balance sheet.

### 2. Request and Approval Process
To ensure client deliverables are not compromised, all PTO requests must be submitted via the *Workday* portal at least 14 business days in advance for absences exceeding three consecutive days. 

Approval is subject to "Project Coverage Requirements." Managers may deny requests if they coincide with critical project milestones (e.g., Q3 Audit cycles or Final Delivery dates). In such cases, the employee is encouraged to reschedule for the following window.

### 3. Specialized Leave
**A. Sabbatical Leave:** After five years of continuous full-time employment, employees are eligible for a four-week paid sabbatical. This must be taken in a single block and requires approval from the Department Head.

**B. Sick and Wellness Leave:** Employees are granted 7 days of paid sick leave annually. For absences exceeding three consecutive business days, a physician’s note is required to be uploaded to the HR portal for compliance.

**C. Bereavement:** Fenwick provides 3 days of paid leave for the loss of an immediate family member.

### 4. Unpaid Leave and Compliance
Leaves of absence exceeding 30 days (including FMLA or personal leave) require a formal written application to the Director of People Operations. During unpaid leave, benefits contributions will be transitioned to the employee's responsibility via direct billing to maintain coverage continuity.

Failure to return on the agreed-upon date without prior communication will be treated as voluntary resignation from Fenwick Analytics.
