# Fenwick Analytics: Professional Conduct & Data Ethics Guidelines

**Document ID:** HR-POL-2024-08  
**Owner:** Department of People & Culture  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Classification:** Confidential – Internal Use Only

### 1. Overview and Scope
As a leader in predictive data analytics, Fenwick Analytics relies on the absolute integrity of our analysts and engineers. These guidelines establish the standard for professional behavior and the ethical handling of proprietary information. Compliance is mandatory for all full-time employees and contracted consultants.

### 2. Client Data Privacy & "Clean Room" Protocols
The core of our business is trust. Any breach of client confidentiality—regardless of intent—is grounds for immediate termination. 
*   **Data Isolation:** Analysts must only access datasets within the designated project environment assigned to their current sprint. Cross-project data blending without written approval from the Lead Data Architect is strictly prohibited.
*   **Handling PII:** When performing quality assurance on raw feeds, employees must use masked data. Under no circumstances should unmasked PII be stored on local drives or transmitted via Slack/Teams. 

For example, if an analyst discovers a leak in a client ingestion pipeline containing records like *Marcus Thorne (m.thorne_test@example-mail.net | ID: 992-01-XXXX)*, they must not screenshot the error for reporting. Instead, they are required to log a ticket via the Jira "Security-Incident" board and notify the Data Privacy Officer immediately.

### 3. Workplace Interaction & Communication
Fenwick maintains a high-performance environment that values rigorous intellectual debate but prohibits harassment or exclusionary behavior.
*   **Peer Reviews:** Code reviews and model audits are intended to be objective. Feedback must focus on the logic, efficiency, and accuracy of the work, not the individual. 
*   **Communication Channels:** All internal communication regarding project deliverables must occur within the encrypted Fenwick ecosystem. The use of personal WhatsApp or Telegram groups for business coordination is a violation of our security protocol.

### 4. Conflict of Interest
Employees may not engage in freelance data consulting or provide advisory services to any entity that competes with Fenwick’s current client portfolio (refer to the *Current Client Registry* on the HR Portal). Any outside professional activities must be disclosed via Form 12-B and approved by the VP of Operations.

### 5. Enforcement
Violations of these guidelines will be documented in the employee's permanent file. Depending on the severity, disciplinary action may range from a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) to immediate separation.
