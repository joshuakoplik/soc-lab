# Fenwick Analytics: Compensation Bands & Salary Structure Overview

**Document Owner:** Human Resources Department  
**Last Updated:** October 12, 2023  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  

### Purpose and Philosophy
Fenwick Analytics utilizes a structured compensation banding system to ensure internal equity, market competitiveness, and transparent career progression. Our philosophy is to reward technical mastery and leadership impact through a combination of base salary and performance-based bonuses. Each band represents a level of responsibility, required expertise, and expected autonomy within the organization.

### Compensation Band Framework
Salaries are determined by the employee’s role, experience level, and geographic cost-of-labor adjustment (Tier 1: SF/NYC; Tier 2: Austin/Chicago; Tier 3: Remote). The following bands represent our baseline for the Data & Engineering tracks:

| Band | Level | Base Salary Range (Mid-Market) | Target Bonus % |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Band 1** | Associate Analyst | $72,000 – $94,000 | 5% |
| **Band 2** | Data Analyst / Engineer | $95,000 – $130,000 | 10% |
| **Band 3** | Senior Analyst / Engineer | $131,000 – $175,000 | 15% |
| **Band 4** | Principal / Lead Architect | $176,000 – $220,000 | 20% |
| **Band 5** | Director / VP of Analytics | $221,000+ | 25%+ |

### Movement Within Bands
Positioning within a band is determined by "compa-ratio"—the relationship between an employee's actual salary and the midpoint of their band. 

*   **Entry Point:** New hires typically enter at the 25th to 50th percentile of a band based on prior experience.
*   **Merit Increases:** Annual adjustments are reviewed every January. High performers (rated "Exceeds Expectations" in Q4 reviews) are eligible for increases that move them toward the band midpoint or ceiling.
*   **Promotions:** A promotion to a higher band typically triggers a salary increase of 8% to 15%, ensuring the employee enters the new band at a competitive starting point.

### Review Process
Compensation bands are audited bi-annually by HR and the Finance team to ensure Fenwick remains competitive against industry benchmarks (primarily using Radford and Mercer data). If market shifts exceed 5% for a specific role, HR may implement an "out-of-cycle" market adjustment.

For individual inquiries regarding your current standing within your band, please schedule a session with your People Partner via the Zenefits portal.
