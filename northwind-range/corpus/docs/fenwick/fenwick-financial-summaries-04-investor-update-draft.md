# Q3 Fiscal Update: Investor Relations Draft

**Owner:** Finance Department / Office of the CFO  
**Status:** Internal Review Only – Confidential  
**Date:** October 14, 2023

### Executive Summary
Fenwick Analytics has concluded Q3 with a total recurring revenue (ARR) of $14.2M, representing a 12% increase over Q2. While top-line growth remains steady, the primary focus of this quarter was the aggressive stabilization of gross margins following the migration to the AWS Graviton3 instances. We have successfully reduced infrastructure overhead from 22% to 17% of total revenue, effectively offsetting the increased headcount in the Professional Services division.

### Revenue Performance & Churn
Net Revenue Retention (NRR) stands at 104%, though we observed a slight dip in the Mid-Market segment. The loss of the Axiom Logistics account ($210k ARR) due to an internal consolidation was partially mitigated by the expansion of our enterprise contract with Sterling Healthcare, which added $340k in incremental seat licenses for their predictive diagnostics suite. 

Our current pipeline for Q4 is valued at $3.8M in weighted opportunities. The sales cycle has lengthened from 45 to 62 days, primarily due to increased scrutiny on data governance and SOC2 Type II compliance requirements during the procurement phase.

### OpEx and Burn Rate
Operating expenses for Q3 totaled $3.1M. The primary driver was a one-time $450k expenditure related to the acquisition of the "FluxStream" dataset, which will be integrated into our Core Analytics Engine by January. 

Current cash runway is estimated at 22 months based on a monthly burn rate of $820k. We are currently maintaining a cash reserve of $17.4M. To optimize this, Finance is implementing a new procurement cap on non-essential SaaS tooling for the Engineering team, effective November 1st.

### Strategic Outlook
The priority for Q4 is the conversion of our "Beta" cohort (currently 12 firms) into paid annual contracts. We anticipate an additional $600k in ARR from this transition. Additionally, we are evaluating a shift in pricing strategy from per-seat licensing to a consumption-based model for our API endpoints to better capture value from high-volume enterprise users.

**Action Items for Board Review:**
* Approval of the updated 2024 headcount budget (Projected +14 FTEs).
* Final sign-off on the FluxStream amortization schedule.
