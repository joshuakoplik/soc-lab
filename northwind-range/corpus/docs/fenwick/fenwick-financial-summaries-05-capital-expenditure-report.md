# FY24 Q3 Capital Expenditure & Infrastructure Audit

**Owner:** Finance Department / Office of the CFO  
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL – INTERNAL USE ONLY  
**Date:** October 14, 2024

### Executive Summary
This report outlines the capital allocation and depreciation schedules for the third quarter of FY24. Total CapEx spend for Q3 reached $4.2M, representing a 12% variance over the projected budget. This increase is primarily attributed to the accelerated migration of our core analytics engine to the new High-Performance Computing (HPC) cluster in the Northern Virginia data center.

### Infrastructure & Hardware Procurement
The primary driver of expenditure was the $2.8M investment in NVIDIA H100 GPU clusters and associated InfiniBand networking gear. While originally slated for Q4, the procurement was moved forward to accommodate the expanded training requirements of the "Sentinel-V" predictive model. 

*   **Compute Expansion:** $2.8M (Hardware + Installation)
*   **Storage Arrays:** $650K (Pure Storage FlashArray upgrades for low-latency data retrieval)
*   **Workstation Refresh:** $120K (Replacement of legacy terminals for the Quantitative Analysis team)

### Software Capitalization
Following current accounting standards, Fenwick Analytics has capitalized $480K in internal development costs associated with the "Project Prism" dashboard. This reflects 4,200 man-hours of engineering time dedicated to the creation of a proprietary data visualization layer that will be offered as a premium tier for Enterprise clients starting Q1 FY25.

### Depreciation and Asset Lifecycle
The Finance department has adjusted the useful life of our legacy Dell PowerEdge servers from five years to three, resulting in an accelerated depreciation charge of $210K this quarter. This adjustment reflects the rapid obsolescence of these units given the shift toward GPU-accelerated workloads.

### Q4 Forecast and Variance Mitigation
To offset the Q3 overspend, Finance is implementing a freeze on non-essential hardware refreshes for the remaining two months of the fiscal year. We are currently renegotiating the service level agreement (SLA) with Equinix to reduce monthly recurring colocation costs by an estimated 8%, which will help stabilize the operational budget moving into FY25.
