# Account Cancellation & Subscription FAQ

**Document Owner:** Support Department  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2023

This guide provides clear instructions on how to manage your Fenwick Analytics subscription and outlines what happens to your data upon cancellation.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your plan directly through the **Billing Portal**. Navigate to *Settings > Subscription > Manage Plan* and select "Cancel Subscription." Once confirmed, you will receive a confirmation email from our billing system. 

For Enterprise accounts with dedicated Account Managers, please submit a cancellation request via the Support Ticket portal or contact your representative directly.

### Will I get a refund for the remainder of my term?
Fenwick Analytics operates on a no-refund policy for partial billing cycles. If you cancel mid-month or mid-year, you will retain full access to all premium features until the end of your current billing period. Your account will then automatically transition to our "Read-Only" Free Tier.

### What happens to my data after I cancel?
We prioritize data integrity and provide a grace period for departing clients:
*   **The 30-Day Window:** For 30 days following the end of your paid term, your dashboards and datasets remain intact in "Read-Only" mode. You can export your raw CSVs and JSON configuration files during this time.
*   **Permanent Deletion:** After 30 days, all proprietary datasets and custom API integrations are permanently purged from our production servers to ensure security and privacy compliance.

### Can I pause my account instead of cancelling?
Yes. If you are experiencing a seasonal lull in data needs, you may request a **Subscription Pause**. This freezes your billing for up to 90 days while preserving all your custom configurations and historical snapshots. To initiate a pause, please open a ticket with the Support team.

### How do I remove my API keys?
While cancelling your subscription disables active data streams, we recommend manually revoking your API keys via the *Developer Console* before your account expires to ensure no further unauthorized calls are attempted by external third-party integrations.

**Need more help?**  
If you are cancelling due to a technical hurdle or pricing concerns, please contact our Customer Success team at support@fenwickanalytics.com before finalizing your request.
