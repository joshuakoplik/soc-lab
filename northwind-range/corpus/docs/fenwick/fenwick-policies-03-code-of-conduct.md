# Fenwick Analytics: Employee Code of Conduct & Professional Standards

**Document Owner:** Administration Department  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

### 1. Data Integrity and Ethical Handling
As a data analytics firm, our reputation rests on the precision and honesty of our insights. Employees are strictly prohibited from "smoothing" or manipulating datasets to reach a desired conclusion for a client. All analysts must document their methodology in the Fenwick Central Repository (FCR) using the standard `.json` logging format. Any discovery of systemic errors in delivered reports must be reported to the Project Lead within 24 hours of detection.

### 2. Client Confidentiality and "Air-Gapping"
Fenwick Analytics operates under strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). To prevent cross-contamination of client data:
*   **Environment Separation:** Employees must use separate virtual environments for each client project. Never transfer scripts or datasets between different client folders on the shared drive.
*   **Hardware Security:** Laptops must be locked whenever an employee leaves their desk. The use of unencrypted USB drives is strictly prohibited; all file transfers must occur via the secure Fenwick Cloud Gateway.

### 3. Professional Conduct and Communication
We maintain a culture of "Radical Candor" tempered with professional respect. 
*   **Internal Comms:** Slack is our primary tool for rapid coordination, but formal project approvals and client deliverables must be documented via email or Jira tickets to ensure an audit trail.
*   **External Interaction:** Only designated Account Managers are authorized to negotiate contract terms or change the scope of a Statement of Work (SOW). Technical staff should refer all "scope creep" requests to their respective Department Head.

### 4. Workspace and Equipment
Fenwick provides high-performance workstations tailored to data processing needs.
*   **Equipment Care:** Any hardware failure must be logged through the Admin Portal under "IT Support." Unauthorized hardware modifications to company machines are prohibited.
*   **Office Etiquette:** In our open-plan office, we observe "Deep Work" hours from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM daily. During this window, please limit non-urgent verbal interruptions and use Slack for asynchronous communication.

### 5. Compliance and Reporting
Violations of this code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees who witness unethical data practices or security breaches should contact the Admin Department or use the anonymous "Fenwick Ethics Hotline" at extension 402.
