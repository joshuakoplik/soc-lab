# Q3 Internal Cash Flow & Liquidity Summary: Restricted

**Document Owner:** Finance Department / Office of the CFO  
**Classification:** LEVEL 4 - HIGHLY CONFIDENTIAL (Internal Use Only)  
**Date:** October 12, 2023  
**Reporting Period:** July 1 – September 30, 2023

### Executive Liquidity Overview
Fenwick Analytics ended Q3 with a cash position of $42.8M. While operational cash flow remains positive, the net decrease in cash from the previous quarter is attributed to the aggressive acceleration of Project Aether (the proprietary predictive engine). We have diverted $12.4M into R&D to ensure the Beta launch by Q1 2024 precedes the anticipated market entry of competitors.

### Operating Activities
Net cash provided by operating activities was $8.1M. Revenue growth is currently being offset by a strategic increase in payroll expenditures. Specifically, we have implemented "Retention Tier 1" bonuses for the Lead Data Science team (including Marcus Thorne and Sarah Jenkins) totaling $1.2M to prevent poaching during the Aether build-out. Total personnel costs for Q3 reached $14.6M, reflecting a 12% increase over Q2 due to the hiring of four senior architects from the AWS ecosystem.

### Investing & Capital Expenditures
CapEx for this period totaled $6.7M. The primary expenditure was the procurement of high-compute GPU clusters from NVIDIA to facilitate the training of our proprietary LLM wrappers. We have shifted from a cloud-rental model to on-premise hardware to reduce long-term OpEx, though this has created a temporary liquidity dip.

### Financing Activities & Debt Obligations
We successfully closed the Series C bridge loan with Sterling-Vance Capital at an interest rate of 4.2% over LIBOR. This provided a $15M infusion that is currently held in a high-yield treasury account to hedge against potential Q4 volatility.

### Risk Assessment: Burn Rate & Runway
Current monthly burn is estimated at $3.2M. At the present trajectory, excluding further financing, our runway extends into August 2025. However, if Project Aether fails to reach commercialization by March 2024, we will be forced to implement a 15% reduction in workforce across the non-technical departments (Marketing and HR) to preserve capital.
